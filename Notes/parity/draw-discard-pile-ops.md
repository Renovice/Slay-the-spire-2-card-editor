# draw-discard-pile-ops
## Card pile operations: vanilla-parity audit

Compared the mod's pile-operation effect engine (`CardEditorExtraEffects.cs` cases DrawCards/DrawCardsThatCostLess, DiscardCards, ExhaustCards, MoveCardsBetweenPiles, PlayCardFromPile, ShuffleDrawPile, plus `CardEditorTargetedDiscardPatches.cs` and `CardEditorMovedPileTriggerPatches.cs`) against vanilla `CardPileCmd.cs`, `CardCmd.cs`, `CardSelectCmd.cs`, `Hook.cs`, and reference content (Havoc, Acrobatics/Survivor/DaggerThrow, Anointed, NoDrawPower, ReboundPower).

**Confirmed good parity (not reported):** unfiltered draws route through vanilla `CardPileCmd.Draw`; discard uses `CardCmd.Discard` (history + AfterCardDiscarded + Sly autoplay + InvokeContentsChanged); exhaust uses `CardCmd.Exhaust` (history + AfterCardExhausted + vanilla VFX), including the MoveCardsBetweenPilesâ†’Discard/Exhaust routes; PlayCardFromPile uses vanilla `CardCmd.AutoPlay` (Unplayable respected, no energy spent, random-enemy targeting via Rng.CombatTargets, counts as a card played); hand-size limit matches (`CardPile.MaxCardsInHand` is the constant 10 the mod hardcodes); move-to-draw-pile Top maps to index 0 = next card drawn, matching vanilla draw order; ShuffleDrawPile calls vanilla `CardPileCmd.Shuffle` (discardâ†’draw, AfterShuffle fires); the targeted-discard Harmony patches reproduce the vanilla Acrobatics/Survivor/DaggerThrow OnPlay bodies line-for-line, only parameterizing the discard count.

**Divergences found:** 2 major (filtered draws ignore No Draw; PlayCardFromPile doesn't reshuffle an empty draw pile) and 5 minor (random picks use the Shuffle RNG stream; force-exhaust stomps result-pile modifiers after they've been credited, wasting Rebound stacks; filtered-draw loop misses combat-over checks/NO_DRAW bubble parity; hand-discard choice falls back to a grid selector when the source card is includable; moved-pile triggers run detached and fire during reshuffles).

## [major] Filtered draws bypass Hook.ShouldDraw ("No Draw" effects are ignored)
VANILLA: Every draw goes through CardPileCmd.Draw, which calls Hook.ShouldDraw before drawing and Hook.AfterPreventingDraw when blocked; NoDrawPower returns false and flashes, so the player draws nothing (CardPileCmd.cs:710-714, NoDrawPower.cs:19-31).
MOD: DrawCards/DrawCardsThatCostLess switch to a hand-rolled loop whenever any draw filter (pool/type/rarity/cost/name) is set or the source pile isn't the draw pile (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28613-28620). That loop (28634-28677) adds cards to hand and fires History.CardDrawn/Hook.AfterCardDrawn but never calls Hook.ShouldDraw â€” grep confirms 'ShouldDraw' appears nowhere in the mod. Only the unfiltered path (28617) routes through vanilla CardPileCmd.Draw.
SCENARIO: Player is under a Battle Trance-style No Draw power and plays a mod card configured 'Draw 2 Attack cards'. The mod draws both cards (and even fires on-draw triggers); the same card without a filter ('Draw 2') is correctly blocked. Vanilla would prevent the draw and flash the power either way.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28606-28681 (no ShouldDraw call) vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:710-714 and ...\src\Core\Models\Powers\NoDrawPower.cs:19-31

## [major] PlayCardFromPile never reshuffles an empty draw pile before playing
VANILLA: CardPileCmd.AutoPlayFromDrawPile (used by Havoc, Cascade, Mayhem, Distilled Chaos) calls ShuffleIfNecessary before each pick, so an empty draw pile is refilled from the discard pile and the card still plays (CardPileCmd.cs:822-857, Havoc.cs:21).
MOD: PlayCardsFromPile builds its candidate list from the pile's current contents via GetCandidatesFromConfiguredPile and silently returns when it's empty (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:30011-30028); no ShuffleIfNecessary call exists anywhere in the path, and the whole selection is taken up-front so mid-effect depletion is never refilled either.
SCENARIO: A mod Havoc clone ('Play the top card of your draw pile; it exhausts') is played with 0 cards in the draw pile and 20 in the discard pile: vanilla Havoc triggers a reshuffle and plays a card; the mod card visibly does nothing (energy and the card are still spent). Similarly 'play the top 2 cards' with 1 card left plays only 1 instead of reshuffling and playing 2.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:30011-30028 and 29537-29596 vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:830-846 (ShuffleIfNecessary per pick) and ...\src\Core\Models\Cards\Havoc.cs:21

## [minor] Random card picks drain the Shuffle RNG stream instead of CombatCardSelection
VANILLA: Random picks from piles use RunState.Rng.CombatCardSelection (CardPileCmd.cs:837 for AutoPlayFromDrawPile Random, Anointed.cs:23, AggressionPower.cs:28); Rng.Shuffle is consumed only by shuffle ordering (CardPileCmd.cs:777).
MOD: All pile ops pass owner.RunState?.Rng?.Shuffle into SelectCardsFromCandidates (MoveCardsBetweenPiles 28733, DiscardCards 29111, ExhaustCards 29165, PlayCardsFromPile 30057), and Random/RandomOffer modes feed it to PickRandomDistinct (28459-28476, 30323-30343).
SCENARIO: In a seeded run, playing a mod card with 'discard 1 random card' advances the shuffle stream, so the next reshuffle produces a different draw order than the same seed in vanilla; the random pick itself also never matches what an equivalent vanilla random effect would choose. Determinism-sensitive players (seed racing, replays) see the divergence directly.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:29111, 28733, 30057, 30323-30343 vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:777 and 837, ...\src\Core\Models\Cards\Anointed.cs:23

## [minor] Force-exhaust is applied AFTER vanilla result-pile modifiers â€” Rebound/Nostalgia stacks consumed for nothing

SCENARIO: With Rebound active, playing a mod card 'Play the top card of your draw pile; it exhausts' visibly flashes Rebound and removes one stack while the auto-played card exhausts anyway. Vanilla Havoc under Rebound leaves the stack untouched.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:26-44 and CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:30081-30084 vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:851, ...\src\Core\Models\CardModel.cs:1522-1526 and 1687-1698, ...\src\Core\Models\Powers\ReboundPower.cs:19-38

## [minor] Filtered draw loop ignores combat-over state and changes the NO_DRAW bubble rule
VANILLA: CardPileCmd.Draw aborts pre-loop and per-iteration when CombatManager.IsOverOrEnding (CardPileCmd.cs:706, 736) and shows the NO_DRAW thought bubble whenever draw+discard piles are both empty (942-948).
MOD: The custom DrawMatchingCards loop (28634-28677) has no IsOverOrEnding check at all, and shows NO_DRAW only when draw+discard+exhaust+deck are ALL empty (28661-28668); when piles have cards but none match the filter it silently does nothing.
SCENARIO: Mid 'Draw 3 Skills', the first drawn card's on-draw trigger kills the last enemy: the mod keeps dealing the remaining cards into the hand during the combat-end sequence. Separately, when the only remaining cards sit in the exhaust pile, vanilla shows the 'no cards to draw' bubble but the mod's filtered draw gives no feedback.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28634-28677 vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:706, 730-748, 942-955

## [minor] Hand discard selection falls back to a grid picker when 'include this card' is enabled

MOD: SelectCardsFromCandidates only uses FromHandForDiscard when sourceCard==null or the source card is excluded (canUseHandSelector, CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28507-28519); with IncludeSourceCardInSelection set (28344-28354) hand discards route through CardSelectCmd.FromSimpleGrid (28522) â€” a modal grid screen no vanilla hand-discard ever uses, which also skips the Sly gold-glow hinting.
SCENARIO: A power-hosted 'at end of turn: discard 1 card (this card selectable)' pops a full-screen grid listing the hand instead of the vanilla in-hand picking UI, and Sly cards aren't highlighted.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28507-28522 and 28344-28354 vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardSelectCmd.cs:580-593 and ...\src\Core\Models\Cards\Acrobatics.cs:24

## [minor] Moved-to-top/bottom pile triggers run detached from the hook pipeline and fire during reshuffles
VANILLA: Hook.AfterCardChangedPiles listeners are awaited strictly in sequence before the pile operation continues (Hook.cs:112-118, CardPileCmd.cs:556-563); during Shuffle, cards already in the draw pile are re-added silently (AddInternal, CardPileCmd.cs:812) while discard-pile cards go through Add and fire the hook per card (797).
MOD: The Postfix on Hook.AfterCardChangedPiles launches the OnMovedToTopOfPile/OnMovedToBottomOfPile effects via TaskHelper.RunSafely without awaiting (CardEditorMovedPileTriggerPatches.cs:35), so they race the original effect's remaining steps; and because each reshuffled discard card momentarily sits at the pile bottom (and the first one at the top), the triggers fire from ordinary reshuffles â€” but only for discard-origin cards, never for draw-pile residents.
SCENARIO: A card with 'when moved to the bottom of your draw pile: gain 2 Block' gains block in the middle of every reshuffle it returns from the discard pile, with the block applying interleaved/out of order with the effect that caused the shuffle â€” and inconsistently depending on which pile the card happened to be in.
EVIDENCE: mod: ...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorMovedPileTriggerPatches.cs:20-77 vs vanilla: ...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Hooks\Hook.cs:112-118 and ...\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:793-814

### VERDICT [confirmed] Filtered draws bypass Hook.ShouldDraw ("No Draw" effects are ignored)
CONFIRMED (major). Both sides of the evidence verified, and no compensating mod code exists.

VANILLA: CardPileCmd.Draw (...\Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:710-714) gates every draw with Hook.ShouldDraw and calls Hook.AfterPreventingDraw when blocked. Hook.ShouldDraw (src\Core\Hooks\Hook.cs:1808-1812) polls all models; NoDrawPower.ShouldDraw (src\Core\Models\Powers\NoDrawPower.cs:19-31) flashes and returns false for non-hand draws by its owner (the Fiddle relic, src\Core\Models\Relics\Fiddle.cs:24, is a second affected modifier). Critically, a project-wide grep shows the ONLY vanilla code that fires History.CardDrawn / Hook.AfterCardDrawn / InvokeDrawn is CardPileCmd.cs:756-758 â€” i.e., in vanilla every event that counts as a "draw" passes the ShouldDraw gate first; there is no vanilla filtered-draw path that bypasses it.

MOD: DrawMatchingCards (...\mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28606-28681) switches to custom routing whenever HasCardSelectionCriteria(effect) is true (pool/type/rarity/count-filter/match-mode/cost/name â€” CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:30884-30894) or the source pile isn't the draw pile (28613-28614). The custom loop (28634-28677) adds cards to hand via CardPileCmd.Add, fires History.CardDrawn, Hook.AfterCardDrawn (fromHandDraw: false) and InvokeDrawn (28673-28676), but never calls Hook.ShouldDraw or Hook.AfterPreventingDraw. Grep confirms "ShouldDraw" appears nowhere in the mod. Both call sites â€” DrawCards (26893-26901) and DrawCardsThatCostLess (26903-26922) â€” invoke it with no guard. No Harmony patch compensates: the only draw-adjacent patches are on Hook.AfterCardDrawn and Hook.BeforeHandDraw (CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:629, 983) and NoDrawPower turn-end duration patches (CardEditorNoDrawDurationPatches.cs:14-49); none re-add the ShouldDraw gate. The duration patches actually make this worse in practice: the mod actively supports editor cards that apply NoDrawPower (e.g., Battle Trance / Bullet Time variants per NCardEditorPopup.cs:11548), so the collision is realistic, not theoretical.

SCENARIO VERIFIED: Under No Draw, a mod card "Draw 2 Attack cards" takes the filtered path (CardGeneratedCardType != Any) and draws both cards â€” and because the loop fires AfterCardDrawn/InvokeDrawn with fromHandDraw:false, it also triggers downstream on-draw powers (Confused, Pagestorm, Void, etc.) on draws that should never have happened. The identical card with no filter routes through CardPileCmd.Draw (28617) and is correctly blocked with the power flash. Same asymmetry applies to DrawCardsThatCostLess and to any draw sourced from discard/exhaust/deck piles. Severity: major â€” a core debuff (No Draw) and the Fiddle relic are silently ignored by an entire class of mod effects, with visible wrong outcomes (cards drawn, on-draw triggers fired, no prevention flash).

### VERDICT [confirmed] PlayCardFromPile never reshuffles an empty draw pile before playing
CONFIRMED (major). Vanilla: CardPileCmd.AutoPlayFromDrawPile (Slay the spire 2 Source\src\Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:822-857) calls ShuffleIfNecessary at line 832 inside the per-pick loop; ShuffleIfNecessary (859-868) refills an empty draw pile from discard, so Havoc (Havoc.cs:21), Cascade, Mayhem, Distilled Chaos, and I Am Invincible all reshuffle before each pick, including mid-effect. Mod: PlayCardsFromPile (mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:30011) snapshots candidates once via GetCandidatesFromConfiguredPile (30018-30024, definition 29537-29596 â€” a pure read of current pile contents) and silently returns when empty (30025-30028); count is clamped to the snapshot (Math.Min at 30040), so mid-effect depletion is also never refilled. Refutation attempts failed: the ONLY ShuffleIfNecessary call in the mod is at CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:28644 inside DrawMatchingCards (the Draw effect), proving the author uses the helper elsewhere but not here; the mod never calls vanilla AutoPlayFromDrawPile (zero hits); a mod-wide grep for Shuffle hits only CardEditorExtraEffects.cs and NCardEditorPopup.cs (UI), and no Harmony patch in CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs compensates; PlayCardFromPile (kind 51, dispatched at 27496-27500, power-hosted variant at 35842) is the only Havoc-style building block. Both scenarios hold: with 0 draw / 20 discard the mod card spends energy and does nothing where vanilla reshuffles and plays; 'play top 2' with 1 card left plays 1 instead of reshuffling to play 2. Minor caveat (does not weaken claim): vanilla also no-ops when draw AND discard are both empty (CardPileCmd.cs:840-843), so divergence requires a non-empty discard, as the scenario states.
