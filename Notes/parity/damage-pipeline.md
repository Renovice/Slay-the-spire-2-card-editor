# damage-pipeline
Compared the mod's DealDamage execution pipeline (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs: main AttackCommand block ~26660-26772, ExecuteDynamicResultRepeatDamage 26229-26294, ExecuteOtherEnemiesDamage 26296-26353, ExecutePerTargetConditionalDamage 26364-26435, and the VigorPreserver hack 12517-12720) against vanilla AttackCommand.Execute, AttackContext, CreatureCmd.Damage, VigorPower, GigantificationPower, StrengthPower, and the vanilla card precedents Omnislice (echo damage = Unpowered, AttackContext+CreatureCmd.Damage) and EchoingSlash (repeat-on-kill loop inside one AttackContext).

Parity points verified GOOD (not reported): per-hit random target re-roll (mod uses vanilla TargetingRandomOpponents via reflection compat; dynamic path re-resolves per repeat with the same Rng.CombatTargets stream); Strength applied per hit in all paths (per CreatureCmd.Damage call, same as vanilla); thorns fires in all paths (ThornsPower hooks BeforeDamageReceived inside CreatureCmd.Damage); Hook.BeforeAttack/AfterAttack fire on the direct-CreatureCmd.Damage paths via the vanilla AttackContext wrapper, exactly the pattern Omnislice/EchoingSlash use; Hook.ModifyAttackHitCount is not consulted by the helper paths but has zero vanilla implementers and vanilla loop-cards (EchoingSlash) skip it too; History.CreatureAttacked is skipped by the helper paths but its only vanilla consumers (Flatten, Rattle) count Osty attacks, and the mod's Osty path uses real AttackCommand.Execute; attacker-death mid-loop in the dynamic path matches EchoingSlash's behavior (CreatureCmd.Damage dead-dealer early return).

Real divergences found: 4 major (Strength/Vigor double-dip on result-derived damage in the main path; "Take X damage" executed as a powered blockable self-attack; one Gigantification stack burned per damage row; Vigor silently consumed by Unpowered echo rows) and 3 minor (visible Vigor flicker from the restore hack; per-target AoE halting after thorns kills the attacker; missing attack animation on the dynamic-repeat path).

## [major] Result-derived ("damage equal to...") rows get Strength/Vigor added AGAIN on single-target/AllEnemies/RandomEnemy, double-dipping
VANILLA: When vanilla re-deals already-dealt damage it marks the hit Unpowered so Strength/Vigor are not applied a second time: Omnislice deals its echo damage with ValueProp.Unpowered | ValueProp.Move, and StrengthPower/VigorPower return 0 for non-powered attacks.
MOD: ExecuteOtherEnemiesDamage and ExecutePerTargetConditionalDamage correctly set ValueProp.Unpowered | ValueProp.Move when the amount comes from a damage result (UsesDamageResultAmountSource). But the MAIN path (target = chosen enemy, AllEnemies without per-target conditional, RandomEnemy, Self) builds DamageCmd.Attack(amount).FromCard().WithHitCount() with default powered props and never calls .Unpowered(), even though `amount` was resolved from AppliedEffectHpDamage/TotalDamage etc. (which already included Strength/Vigor when the source row dealt it).
SCENARIO: With 5 Strength, play a custom card "Deal 10 damage. Then deal damage equal to the HP damage dealt to the target.": row 1 hits for 15; the echo row's base becomes 15 and Strength is added again, hitting for 20. Change the echo's target to "other enemies" and it deals a flat 15 â€” the same card text behaves differently per target, and both differ from vanilla Omnislice, which never re-adds Strength to echoed damage.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26711 (powered DamageCmd.Attack, no Unpowered), 26355-26362 (UsesDamageResultAmountSource only consulted by helpers), 26312-26314 and 26381-26383 (helpers DO set Unpowered), 25873-25904 (amount resolved from prior damage results). Vanilla: Omnislice.cs:39 (ValueProp.Unpowered | ValueProp.Move for echo damage), StrengthPower.cs:21-25 and VigorPower.cs:63-66 (no bonus unless IsPoweredAttack).

## [major] "Take X damage" (DealDamage with Self target) executes as a powered, blockable attack on yourself
VANILLA: Vanilla self-damage is never powered: "take damage" cards (Hemokinesis, Offering, Bloodletting, etc.) use DamageProps.cardHpLoss = Unblockable | Unpowered | Move, and even blockable self-damage (Burn) is Unpowered | Move. Strength, Vigor and Gigantification never modify it (all require IsPoweredAttack), and HP-loss style self-damage ignores Block.
MOD: The DealDamage Self branch routes through a full AttackCommand: attack.Targeting(ownerCreature) with default DamageProps = ValueProp.Move (powered, blockable). The card text renders as "Take {X} damage." exactly like vanilla HP-loss cards. Note the mod's separate LoseHp kind DOES use DamageProps.cardHpLoss correctly â€” only DealDamageâ†’Self diverges.
SCENARIO: Custom card "Deal 8 damage. Take 3 damage." with 5 Strength and 4 Vigor: the self-hit becomes 3+5+4 = 12 instead of 3, and Vigor/Gigantification get latched and consumed by hitting yourself. Conversely with 10 Block the "Take 3 damage" is fully absorbed by your own Block, while vanilla Hemokinesis always costs exactly 3 HP regardless of Block.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26727-26733 (Self â†’ attack.Targeting(ownerCreature), powered AttackCommand), 22932 (renders "Take X damage."), 26803 (LoseHp correctly uses DamageProps.cardHpLoss). Vanilla: Hemokinesis.cs:28 / Offering.cs:32 (Unblockable|Unpowered|Move), DamageProps.cs:9, Burn.cs:18 (Unpowered|Move), AttackCommand.cs:82 (default DamageProps = ValueProp.Move), StrengthPower.cs:21-25.

## [major] Multi-row damage cards burn one Gigantification stack PER row (Vigor is hack-preserved, sibling 'next attack' powers are not)
VANILLA: Vanilla "next attack" powers latch one AttackCommand in BeforeAttack and pay once in AfterAttack: GigantificationPower triples every hit of the latched command and decrements ONE stack per attack; loop cards like EchoingSlash wrap their entire multi-batch resolution in a single AttackContext, so one card play fires BeforeAttack/AfterAttack exactly once and consumes exactly one stack while tripling all hits. No vanilla card consumes two stacks in one play.
MOD: Each DealDamage row of a custom card executes its own AttackCommand (or its own AttackContext in the helper paths), so each row fires BeforeAttack/AfterAttack separately. The VigorPreserver hack explicitly re-applies consumed Vigor between rows so the whole card behaves like one multi-hit attack for Vigor â€” but no equivalent exists for Gigantification (or any other latched power), so each row latches and decrements a fresh stack.
SCENARIO: With 2 stacks of Gigantification, play a custom card "Deal 6 damage. Deal 6 damage.": both rows are tripled and BOTH stacks vanish in one card play. A vanilla 2-hit attack (one AttackCommand with hit count 2) triples both hits and consumes only one stack. With 1 stack, row 1 deals 18 and row 2 deals 6 â€” while the same card's Vigor bonus (per the mod's own preserver) applies to both rows, an internally inconsistent model.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:12549-12586 (rows executed as separate effects; Vigor-only restore at 12576-12585), 26660-26666 comment + 26711-26752 (one AttackCommand per row), 26247/26316/26385 (one AttackContext per row in helper paths). Vanilla: GigantificationPower.cs:28-85 (latch once, decrement once per command), AttackContext.cs:30-58 (one BeforeAttack/AfterAttack per context), EchoingSlash.cs:25-33 (whole repeat loop inside a single context).

## [major] Unpowered echo rows still consume Vigor (AttackContext dummy command is powered while the real hits are not)
VANILLA: VigorPower.BeforeAttack refuses to latch onto attacks that are not powered (checks command.DamageProps.IsPoweredAttack()), so a vanilla Unpowered attack (AttackCommand.Unpowered(), or Omnislice's echo batch where the powered first hit already justified consumption) never wastes Vigor without granting its bonus.
MOD: ExecuteOtherEnemiesDamage / ExecutePerTargetConditionalDamage deal their result-derived hits with ValueProp.Unpowered (so Vigor correctly adds nothing), but they wrap the loop in AttackCommand.CreateContextAsync, whose internal dummy command is `new AttackCommand(0m).FromCard(card).TargetingAllOpponents()` with default powered props. VigorPower.BeforeAttack latches the powered dummy, and at context dispose VigorPower.AfterAttack consumes the full Vigor amount even though no hit received the bonus.
SCENARIO: Gain 4 Vigor, then play a single-row custom card "Deal damage equal to the HP damage dealt by your last attack to other enemies.": the enemies take the flat echo damage (no +4, correctly), but your 4 Vigor silently disappears anyway â€” a pure loss with zero benefit. In vanilla, an unpowered attack leaves Vigor untouched for your next real attack.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26312-26316 and 26381-26385 (Unpowered hit props + CreateContextAsync per row), 26331/26412 (unpowered CreatureCmd.Damage). Vanilla: AttackContext.cs:27 (dummy command with default powered ValueProp.Move; see AttackCommand.cs:82), VigorPower.cs:35-38 (no latch unless IsPoweredAttack), VigorPower.cs:79-86 (AfterAttack consumes the latched command's snapshot unconditionally).

## [minor] VigorPreserver restores Vigor via real PowerCmd.Apply/Remove â€” visible counter flicker and spurious power-application events mid-card
VANILLA: Vigor consumption is a single silent PowerCmd.ModifyAmount after the attack finishes; vanilla never re-applies a power mid-card-play, so no extra power-gain popups, VFX, or power-applied hook firings occur during resolution.
MOD: Between damage rows, VigorPreserver.RestoreIfConsumed calls PowerCmd.Apply<VigorPower>(target, need, ...) to put the consumed Vigor back, then FinalizeRestores calls PowerCmd.Remove/ModifyAmount at the end. Each restore is a genuine power application: the Vigor stack visibly drops to 0 and pops back up between rows, and anything listening to power application (mod custom "when you gain a buff" triggers, power-applied hooks) fires for phantom Vigor gains the player never earned.
SCENARIO: With 4 Vigor, play a 3-row damage card: the buff bar shows Vigor 4 â†’ 0 â†’ 4 â†’ 0 â†’ 4 â†’ gone within one card resolution, with power-gain feedback each time; a custom enemy/status configured to react "whenever a buff is applied" triggers twice extra during the play. A vanilla multi-hit attack shows a single 4 â†’ 0 tick after the attack.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:12667-12690 (RestoreIfConsumed â†’ PowerCmd.Apply), 12692-12719 (FinalizeRestores â†’ PowerCmd.Remove/ModifyAmount). Vanilla: VigorPower.cs:79-86 (single ModifyAmount after the latched attack, no re-application).

## [minor] Per-target conditional AoE damage stops hitting remaining enemies if thorns kills the attacker mid-sequence
VANILLA: An AoE attack resolves all targets inside ONE CreatureCmd.Damage call: dealer.IsDead is checked once at entry, and the per-target loop keeps damaging the remaining targets even if retaliation (Thorns via BeforeDamageReceived) kills the attacker partway through the batch. AttackCommand passes the whole validTargets list in one call; Omnislice's echo batch does the same.
MOD: ExecutePerTargetConditionalDamage issues a SEPARATE CreatureCmd.Damage call per enemy (to re-evaluate the per-target conditional bonus). Each call re-checks dealer.IsDead at entry, so once thorns kills the player on an earlier enemy, every later enemy takes zero damage and only phantom empty results are recorded.
SCENARIO: At 5 HP, play "Deal 8 damage to ALL enemies. +8 if the target is Poisoned." against three enemies with Thorns 6: enemy #1 takes damage, its thorns kills you, and enemies #2 and #3 take nothing. The equivalent vanilla AoE attack (or the mod's own non-conditional AllEnemies path) would still damage all three in the same batch.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26396-26417 (per-enemy sequential CreatureCmd.Damage calls). Vanilla: CreatureCmd.cs:129-143 (dealer.IsDead checked once, then foreach over all targets), AttackCommand.cs:470 (whole target list passed in one call), Omnislice.cs:39 (batched echo).

## [minor] Repeat-on-result damage plays no attack animation or hit feedback on created cards, unlike the same card without it
VANILLA: Vanilla attack cards animate the attacker per hit through AttackCommand (TriggerAnim + hit VFX/SFX in Execute). The mod's own main DealDamage path deliberately enables the attacker animation for created/custom cards "so the card feels real".
MOD: ExecuteDynamicResultRepeatDamage (taken whenever the row uses ScaleMode RepeatByCount on its own EffectResult, e.g. "repeat for each enemy killed") bypasses AttackCommand entirely and never plays the attacker animation, attack SFX, or hit VFX â€” only the bare damage numbers from CreatureCmd.Damage appear. (Vanilla EchoingSlash shares this pattern, but the divergence is that the identical created card WITHOUT the repeat setting does swing.)
SCENARIO: Build a created Attack card "Deal 6 damage" â€” the character swings on play. Edit the same card to "Deal 6 damage. Repeat for each enemy this kills." â€” the character now stands still while damage numbers tick, looking like no attack happened.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26664-26668 (dynamic path short-circuits before the anim decision), 26671-26673 + 26712-26717 (main path enables attacker anim for created cards), 26229-26293 (dynamic loop has no anim/VFX). Vanilla: AttackCommand.cs:386-394 (attacker anim per hit), EchoingSlash.cs:25-33 (anim-less loop precedent).

### VERDICT [confirmed] Multi-row damage cards burn one Gigantification stack PER row (Vigor is hack-preserved, sibling 'next attack' powers are not)
