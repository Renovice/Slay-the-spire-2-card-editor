# text-rendering-rest
Compared the mod's auto-generated card text (TryFormatLine/FormatLineForAmount and the Format* helpers in `mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs`, keyword/title handling in `CardEditorCreatedCardsTextPatches.cs`, gold-tag + hover-tip inference in `CardEditorVanillaKeywordSupport.cs`) against vanilla conventions (`Slay the spire 2 Source\localization\eng\cards.json`, `CardModel.GetDescriptionForPile`/`Title`, `CardKeywordExtensions.GetCardText`, `EnergyIconsFormatter`, per-card `ExtraHoverTips`).

Confirmed-matching (not reported): "Deal X damage to ALL enemies." phrasing; energy/star icon rendering (1-3 repeated icons, otherwise digit+icon â€” mod `BuildEnergyIcons` 22710 mirrors vanilla `EnergyIconsFormatter` 87); X-cost "X"/"X+N" and "X times"/"twice"/"once" repeat suffixes; keyword lines ("[gold]Title[/gold]" + PERIOD, before/after-description order identical to `CardKeywordOrder`); upgraded title "+"/"+N" (mod patch is byte-for-byte the vanilla logic); "Draw N card(s)", "Heal N HP", "Take N damage", "Gain N [gold]Block[/gold]", apply-debuff wording; auto gold-tagging restores canonical casing, which matches vanilla (vanilla never uses these terms lowercase/untagged).

Found 7 divergences, all in remaining (non-green) text conventions: missing hover tooltips for non-hardcoded powers and custom statuses, un-gilded "Gold" and "Osty", "Orb" suffix on Channel lines, STS1-style Evoke wording, non-vanilla self-scaling phrasing ("When played, ... for this combat"), and word-vs-digit count wording for discard/exhaust.

## [major] Powers applied via ApplyPower and custom statuses get no hover tooltip on the card in hand
VANILLA: Every vanilla card declares a hover tip for each power it references via ExtraHoverTips (e.g. Abrasive: HoverTipFactory.FromPower<DexterityPower>(), FromPower<ThornsPower>()), so hovering the card in hand always shows a tooltip explaining the status it applies.
MOD: The get_HoverTips postfix infers tips only from a hardcoded term cache covering 18 powers (Strength, Dexterity, Focus, Weak, Frail, Vulnerable, Poison, Doom, Constrict, Artifact, Thorns, Regen, Plating, Intangible, Buffer, Vigor, Blur, Ritual) plus keywords/orbs/statics/piles. FormatApplyPower gold-wraps ANY power title (any vanilla PowerModel by id, or a custom status), but no tooltip is generated for powers outside that list. Custom statuses only get a tooltip on the power icon after being applied (PowerModel.get_HoverTips prefix), never on the card in hand. The mod never overrides ExtraHoverTips (zero matches in the mod).
SCENARIO: Create a card with 'Apply 3 Metallicize' (or any custom status like 'Burning Soul'). In hand the text shows '[gold]Metallicize[/gold]' in gold exactly like a vanilla card, but hovering the card shows no tooltip explaining what Metallicize/the custom status does â€” on every vanilla card the applied power's tooltip appears next to the card.

## [minor] 'Gold' currency is not gold-tagged in gain/lose Gold lines
VANILLA: Vanilla always renders the currency as [gold]Gold[/gold]: HAND_OF_GREED 'If [gold]Fatal[/gold], gain {Gold:diff()} [gold]Gold[/gold].', DEBT '... lose {Gold:diff()} [gold]Gold[/gold].'
MOD: GainGold/LoseGold lines are raw interpolations 'Gain {amountText} Gold.' / 'Lose {amountText} Gold.' with no [gold] tags, and 'Gold' is not in the keyword term list so the auto-golding regex never wraps it.
