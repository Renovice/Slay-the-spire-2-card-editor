# enchant-afflict-keywords
Audited vanilla-parity of the card_editor mod's enchantment/affliction/keyword layer against the decompiled StS2 engine.

**Compared:** mod temporary keyword controller (`CardEditorTemporaryKeywordController.cs`), temporary enchantment controller (`CardEditorTemporaryEnchantmentController.cs`), GrantKeywordToPile / EnchantCard effect kinds and Sly duration patches (`CardEditorExtraEffects.cs`, `CardEditorSlyDurationPatches.cs`), affliction fallback patches (`CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs`), and enchantment/affliction override application (`CardEditorOverrides.cs`) â€” versus vanilla `CardModel` play/turn-end flow, `CardCmd.Enchant/Afflict/AutoPlay`, `EnchantmentModel`, the affliction-driving powers (HexPower, GalvanicPower, ChainsOfBindingPower, RingingPower, SmoggyPower, TangledPower, VitalSparkPower), and keyword-granting enchantments (Steady, Goopy, RoyallyApproved, TezcatarasEmber).

**Verified-good paths (not reported):** mod-granted keywords use vanilla `CardCmd.ApplyKeyword`, so Ethereal exhausts via the vanilla `DoTurnEnd` path, Retain works in the vanilla flush, Sly auto-plays via the vanilla `DiscardAndDraw` trigger; mod auto-play (`PlayCardFromPile`) routes through vanilla `CardCmd.AutoPlay`, so Unplayable and `Hook.ShouldPlay` block it exactly like vanilla; "Exhaust until played" resolves correctly because vanilla snapshots the result pile at play start before the grant is removed; ThisTurn keyword/Sly expiry happens at `Hook.AfterTurnEnd`, after the vanilla retain/flush + `EndOfTurnCleanup`, matching vanilla single-turn-flag timing; EnchantCard candidate filtering uses vanilla `CanEnchant` (no illegal re-enchanting of already-enchanted cards); applied enchantments get their vanilla `OnPlay` extras because the vanilla play wrapper invokes `Enchantment.OnPlay`; affliction visuals/overlay fire via `AfflictInternal` â†’ `AfflictionChanged`; created-card auto descriptions append enchant/affliction purple text and keyword lines in vanilla order; Entangled cost emulation matches vanilla's skip-negative-cost rule.

Five divergences found (4 major, 1 minor), all in the affliction fallback layer and the temporary-enchantment clear/reapply design.

## [major] Editor-set Hexed never makes the card Ethereal
VANILLA: The Hexed affliction's entire gameplay effect lives in HexPower: TryModifyKeywordsInCombat adds CardKeyword.Ethereal to every Hexed card of the power owner's player, so a Hexed card always exhausts at end of turn via the normal Ethereal path. The Hexed affliction itself only carries an Ethereal hover tip and self-clears when no HexPower exists.
MOD: The mod patches Hexed.AfterCardEnteredCombat to stop the self-clear so editor-set Hexed persists without HexPower, but adds no replacement for HexPower's keyword injection â€” there is no TryModifyKeywordsInCombat/ModifyKeywordsInCombat handling anywhere in the mod. The card keeps the Hexed affliction and its Ethereal tooltip but never actually gains Ethereal.
SCENARIO: Give a card the Hexed affliction in the editor and start any fight. The card shows the Hexed overlay and its tooltip says it is Ethereal, but at end of turn it just discards normally instead of exhausting. The same card would always exhaust in vanilla, since vanilla Hexed only exists alongside HexPower's Ethereal grant.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs:204-241 (suppresses self-clear, no Ethereal emulation). Vanilla: HexPower.cs:21-32 (TryModifyKeywordsInCombat adds Ethereal to Hexed cards), Hexed.cs:12 (Ethereal hover tip), CombatManager.cs:1015-1022 (Ethereal exhaust at turn end).

## [major] Galvanized fallback only fires on Power cards; vanilla trigger is type-agnostic
VANILLA: GalvanicPower.AfterCardPlayed checks only `cardPlay.Card.Affliction is Galvanized` â€” any card carrying the Galvanized affliction damages its owner when played, regardless of card type (the Power-card restriction in vanilla applies only to which cards get afflicted, not to the trigger).
MOD: CardEditorAfflictionEffects.RunAfterCardPlayed returns early with `if (card.Type != CardType.Power) return;` (comment claims this mirrors the base game), so the self-damage fallback only fires for Power cards. When any creature in the fight has GalvanicPower the mod defers to the vanilla power, which fires for all card types.
SCENARIO: Editor-afflict an Attack with Galvanized 3. In a fight against the enemy that owns GalvanicPower, playing the Attack zaps you for 3 (vanilla power path). In every other fight the identical card deals no self-damage at all, despite the affliction text/tooltip promising damage when played.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs:131-136 (early return for non-Power types), 124-128 (defers to vanilla power when present). Vanilla: GalvanicPower.cs AfterCardPlayed (lines ~50-57: only checks `Affliction is Galvanized`, no type check).

## [major] Expiring temporary enchantments permanently leak their OnEnchant keywords (Retain/Exhaust/Innate/Eternal)
VANILLA: Keyword-granting enchantments add their keyword in OnEnchant via Card.AddKeyword (Steadyâ†’Retain, Goopyâ†’Exhaust, RoyallyApprovedâ†’Innate+Retain, TezcatarasEmberâ†’Eternal). Vanilla enchantments are permanent; ClearEnchantmentInternal/EnchantmentModel.ClearInternal only null out the enchantment reference and never remove keywords, because vanilla never strips an enchantment mid-combat. A card only ever shows/keeps Retain etc. while the enchantment is on it.
MOD: The EnchantCard effect offers ThisTurn/Turns/UntilPlayed durations for every ModelDb enchantment. On expiry, CardEditorTemporaryEnchantmentController.ReapplyCardState calls ClearCardEnchantment â†’ vanilla CardCmd.ClearEnchantment, which removes the purple text/icon but leaves the OnEnchant-added keyword in the card's local keyword set forever. Nothing in the mod removes Steady's Retain, Goopy's Exhaust, RoyallyApproved's Innate+Retain or TezcatarasEmber's Eternal after the grant ends.
SCENARIO: Create a card with 'Enchant a card in hand with Steady â€” this turn'. Play it on a Strike: this turn the Strike is enchanted and retained (correct). Next turn the enchantment text/icon is gone, but the Strike still shows the Retain keyword line and is retained every turn for the rest of combat. With Goopy 'until played', the target keeps Exhaust on every future play (e.g., after being Exhumed) even though the enchantment is long gone.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorTemporaryEnchantmentController.cs:409-414 (ClearCardEnchantment), 381-407 (ReapplyCardState), 214-228 (turn-end expiry), CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27833/28023 (Apply with EnchantmentDuration). Vanilla: Steady.cs:11-15, Goopy.cs:25-28, RoyallyApproved.cs:24-29, TezcatarasEmber.cs:15-19 (OnEnchant AddKeyword), CardModel.cs:1233-1242 (ClearEnchantmentInternal removes no keywords).

## [major] Tainted is the only editor-offered affliction with no fallback behavior â€” silent no-op
VANILLA: Playing a card afflicted with Tainted applies TaintedPower to its owner (VitalSparkPower.AfterCardPlayed), making the player take +X damage from powered attacks; the affliction's extra card text and TaintedPower hover tip advertise this.
MOD: The affliction dropdown lists every ModelDb.DebugAfflictions entry including Tainted, and the mod implements no-power fallbacks for Smog, Bound, Ringing, Entangled, Galvanized and (partially) Hexed â€” but CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches has no Tainted branch at all. Without VitalSparkPower in the fight, playing an editor-Tainted card applies nothing.
SCENARIO: Editor-afflict a Skill with Tainted 4. The card shows the Tainted text and a TaintedPower tooltip promising '+4 damage taken' when played. Playing it does nothing in any fight that doesn't contain the VitalSpark enemy, while every other affliction the same dropdown offers (Smog, Bound, Ringing, Entangled, Galvanized) does work via the mod's fallbacks.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs:44-151 (fallbacks for Smog/Bound/Ringing/Entangled/Galvanized only; no Tainted), NCardEditorPopup.cs:30652 (offers all DebugAfflictions). Vanilla: VitalSparkPower.cs:51-55 (Tainted card played â†’ apply TaintedPower), TaintedPower.cs:18-29 (extra damage taken).

## [minor] Bound fallback counts duplicated plays toward the one-Bound-per-turn limit; vanilla excludes dupes
VANILLA: ChainsOfBindingPower.BeforeCardPlayed returns early when `card.IsDupe`, so a duplicated ('play a copy') play of a Bound card does not set the boundCardPlayed flag â€” the player can still play a real Bound card that turn. The flag also snapshots the state at play time and resets at side turn end.
MOD: The fallback re-derives the limit from history: `CardPlaysStarted.Any(HappenedThisTurn && owner matches && e.CardPlay.Card.Affliction is Bound)`, with no IsDupe filter and evaluated against the card's current affliction state. Dupe plays create CardPlayStartedEntry records and dupes clone the affliction, so a duplicated Bound play blocks the real one.
SCENARIO: With editor-set Bound on two cards and any duplication effect (e.g. an Echo Form-style power that replays your first card as a copy): under vanilla ChainsOfBinding rules the copy's play wouldn't consume the Bound allowance and you could still play your second Bound card; under the mod's fallback the duplicated play already counts, and the second Bound card is unplayable for the turn.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs:71-84 (history Any, no IsDupe filter). Vanilla: ChainsOfBindingPower.cs BeforeCardPlayed (early return on card.IsDupe, ~lines 49-66) and ShouldPlay (~68-79); CardModel.cs:1562 (CardPlayStarted recorded for every play incl. dupes), CardModel.cs:1774-1784 (CreateDupe clones card, removes only Exhaust).

### VERDICT [confirmed] Editor-set Hexed never makes the card Ethereal
Confirmed on both sides with no compensating code found. VANILLA: Hexed.cs is effect-free â€” line 12 only attaches an Ethereal hover tip, and lines 14-26 self-clear the affliction when the owner has no HexPower. The actual gameplay effect lives entirely in HexPower.cs:21-32, whose TryModifyKeywordsInCombat adds CardKeyword.Ethereal to Hexed cards of the power owner's player. The keyword pipeline (CardModel.cs:936 -> Hook.cs:1263-1269) gathers in-combat keywords exclusively from hook listeners' TryModifyKeywordsInCombat, and a source-wide grep confirms only HexPower implements it for Hexed. End-of-turn exhaust at CombatManager.cs:1015-1022 requires the Ethereal keyword to be present. MOD: CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs:204-241 is a Harmony prefix on Hexed.AfterCardEnteredCombat that suppresses the self-clear when no HexPower exists, so editor-set Hexed persists â€” but greps across the entire mod for ModifyKeywords, Ethereal, GetKeywordsWithSources, ShouldEtherealTrigger, and CombatManager patches show zero Ethereal injection or turn-end exhaust emulation. The only other Hexed references are a cosmetic card-finish overlay. Notably, the same file systematically emulates the power-less behavior of every other editor affliction (Entangled cost increase lines 22-42, Smog/Bound/Ringing play prevention lines 64-98, Galvanized self-damage lines 103-151), making the missing Hexed emulation a clear gap. SCENARIO verified: an editor-Hexed card keeps its Hexed overlay and an Ethereal tooltip (Hexed.cs:12) but card.Keywords never contains Ethereal, so the CombatManager.cs:1015 check fails and the card discards normally at end of turn, whereas any vanilla Hexed card (which only exists alongside HexPower) always exhausts. Severity: major â€” visible rules break plus an actively lying tooltip. Fix suggestion: add a Hook.ModifyKeywordsInCombat postfix (mirroring the Entangled pattern) that adds CardKeyword.Ethereal to cards whose Affliction is Hexed when the owner lacks HexPower.

### VERDICT [confirmed] Galvanized fallback only fires on Power cards; vanilla trigger is type-agnostic
CONFIRMED. Vanilla: GalvanicPower.AfterCardPlayed (Core\Models\Powers\GalvanicPower.cs:50-57) checks only `cardPlay.Card.Affliction is Galvanized` before damaging the card owner's creature â€” no card-type check. The Power-type restriction appears only in BeforeCombatStart (line 34) and AfterCardEnteredCombat (line 44), which control which cards GET afflicted, not which trigger the self-damage. The Galvanized affliction model itself (Core\Models\Afflictions\Galvanized.cs) has no behavior, and Hook.AfterCardPlayed (Core\Hooks\Hook.cs:193-209) dispatches to all combat hook listeners, so an enemy-owned GalvanicPower really does fire on player card plays. Mod: CardEditorAfflictionEffects.RunAfterCardPlayed (mods\card_editor\CardEditorAfflictionEffectPatches.cs:103-151) is the mod's ONLY Galvanized behavior path (case-insensitive grep for "galvan" across the mod finds only this plus cosmetic finish/overlay code in CardEditorCardFinishPatches.cs/CardEditorOverrides.cs/NCardEditorPopup.cs). It defers to vanilla when GalvanicPower is on the owner or any enemy (lines 126-130), then early-returns for non-Power cards at lines 133-136 with a comment incorrectly claiming this mirrors base-game trigger behavior. Wiring is via the Hook.AfterCardPlayed Postfix in CardEditorMod.cs:2750-2781; no compensating Harmony patch, controller, or shim exists anywhere else in the mod. The scenario plays out as described: an editor-afflicted Attack/Skill with Galvanized self-damages in a fight containing GalvanicPower (vanilla type-agnostic path) but deals no self-damage in any other fight (mod fallback's Power gate), despite the affliction tooltip. Minor nitpick not affecting the verdict: in the GalvanicPower fight the vanilla damage equals the POWER's Amount (base.Amount), not the affliction's stack count, whereas the mod fallback uses galvanized.Amount â€” so "zaps you for 3" is only exact if the enemy power's amount is 3; this amount-source mismatch is itself a secondary parity inconsistency. Severity of the confirmed divergence: major (affliction visibly dead in most fights for non-Power cards).

### VERDICT [confirmed] Expiring temporary enchantments permanently leak their OnEnchant keywords (Retain/Exhaust/Innate/Eternal)
CONFIRMED â€” major. The leak is real and the cited evidence on both sides checks out exactly.

VANILLA: CardCmd.Enchant (Core\Commands\CardCmd.cs:419-446) calls card.EnchantInternal + enchantment.ModifyCard(); ModifyCard (Core\Models\EnchantmentModel.cs:306-315) invokes OnEnchant(). Steady.cs:11-14 adds Retain, Goopy.cs:25-28 adds Exhaust, RoyallyApproved.cs:24-28 adds Innate+Retain, TezcatarasEmber.cs:15-19 adds Eternal â€” all via Card.AddKeyword, which mutates the card's persistent LocalKeywords HashSet (CardModel.cs:1074-1078, _keywords at :62/424-434). Clearing is purely referential: CardModel.ClearEnchantmentInternal (CardModel.cs:1233-1242) calls EnchantmentModel.ClearInternal (EnchantmentModel.cs:300-304), which only nulls _card; no keyword is ever removed because vanilla never strips an enchantment mid-combat. FinalizeUpgradeInternal (CardModel.cs:1730-1735), which the mod calls after clearing, only finalizes DynamicVars/EnergyCost and never touches _keywords (only DowngradeInternal at :1737-1750 rebuilds keywords, and the mod never calls it). The leaked keywords drive real rules: Retain at CardModel.cs:490, Exhaust at :1693/:1710, Eternal via IsRemovable at :584.

MOD: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27833 and :28023 route the EnchantCard effect (durations ThisTurn/Turns/UntilPlayed/ThisCombat/Permanent, enum at :648, labels :4854-4862) to CardEditorTemporaryEnchantmentController.Apply (CardEditorTemporaryEnchantmentController.cs:89-143). The per-card snapshot stores only enchantment Id+Amount (lines 14-18, 367-379) â€” no keyword baseline. Application goes through ApplySnapshot â†’ CardCmd.Enchant (line 445), so OnEnchant fires and adds the keyword. On expiry (turn-end at 214-228, until-played at 171-178) ReapplyCardState (381-407) â†’ ClearCardEnchantment (409-414) calls only CardCmd.ClearEnchantment + FinalizeUpgradeInternal + InvokeEnergyCostChanged â€” nothing removes the OnEnchant-added keyword, so Retain/Exhaust/Innate/Eternal stay in LocalKeywords for the rest of combat.

REFUTATION ATTEMPTS (all failed): (1) No Harmony patch anywhere in the mod targets ClearEnchantment/ClearEnchantmentInternal (grep over mods\card_editor). (2) The keyword-sync in CardEditorOverrides.cs:664-681 only runs during override application for editor cards whose definition explicitly sets a Keywords override â€” it never runs on enchantment expiry and never on a vanilla card like Strike. (3) CardEditorMod.cs:576-598 is upgrade-definition keyword add/remove, unrelated. (4) CardEditorTemporaryKeywordController only removes keywords IT granted via its own temp-keyword feature; it does not track OnEnchant keywords. (5) Vanilla offers no rebuild either: GetKeywordsWithSources unions LocalKeywords into the result (CardModel.cs:910-938), so the stale entry always shows.

SCENARIO VERIFIED: 'Enchant with Steady â€” this turn' on a Strike: grant expiry clears the enchantment reference (purple text/icon gone) but Retain remains in LocalKeywords, so CardModel.cs:490 retains the Strike every turn for the rest of combat, with the Retain keyword line still rendered. Goopy 'until played': the post-play expiry (OnAfterCardPlayed, lines 145-179) clears the enchantment but Exhaust persists, so the card exhausts on every future play (e.g., after Exhume) â€” CardModel.cs:1693. RoyallyApproved leaks Retain (visible) + Innate (mostly cosmetic mid-combat), TezcatarasEmber leaks Eternal (card becomes permanently un-removable, IsRemovable at CardModel.cs:584).

ADDITIONAL FINDING (same bug class, worth fixing together): SoulsPower.OnEnchant REMOVES Exhaust (Core\Models\Enchantments\SoulsPower.cs:25-28), so a temporary SoulsPower grant permanently strips Exhaust from the target after expiry â€” the inverse leak. Likely also TezcatarasEmber's cost-to-0 (EnergyCost.UpgradeBy at TezcatarasEmber.cs:17) survives expiry since FinalizeUpgradeInternal bakes the cost change in, though the claim under review did not assert this. The proper fix is to snapshot card keywords (and ideally energy-cost state) in CardState when the first grant is applied and restore them in ReapplyCardState, rather than relying on ClearEnchantment.
