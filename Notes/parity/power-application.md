# power-application
Compared the card_editor mod's status-application engine (ExecuteEffectCore Apply*/Gain*/Remove* cases, ApplyPowerWithDuration/TrackBuiltInPowerDuration/ReducePowerWithTemporaryTracking in CardEditorExtraEffects.cs, CardEditorCustomStatusPower + the CardEditorPowerCmdCompat reflection shim, and the ThisTurn/boundary tracker powers) against vanilla PowerCmd.Apply/ModifyAmount/Remove (Core\\Commands\\PowerCmd.cs:33-216), PowerModel internals (GetTypeForAmount/ShouldRemoveDueToAmount/ApplyInternal), ArtifactPower, UnsettlingLamp, WeakPower/VulnerablePower duration ticks, and vanilla debuff-enumeration patterns (Misery, IllusionPower).

Good news first: all mod apply/stack paths route through real PowerCmd.Apply/ModifyAmount, so the standard hook chain (BeforePowerAmountChanged, ModifyPowerAmountGiven/Received, AfterApplied/AfterPowerAmountChanged, Debuffer badge, Sleight-of-Flesh-style triggers) fires; stacking honors FindExistingInstanceForStacking; partial stack removal uses ModifyAmount; full removal via PowerCmd.Remove matches vanilla precedent (Expose). The compat shim's injected ThrowingPlayerChoiceContext matches vanilla's own usage for monster/anonymous applications.

Divergences found (4 major / 4 minor): (1) custom Debuff statuses pierce Artifact on first application because their Buff->Debuff type is set in BeforeApplied, after Artifact's hook - and restacks ARE blocked, so it's inconsistent both with vanilla and itself; (2) custom-status behavior effects keep executing after the status is removed by duration expiry or stack loss (only the RemovePower path cleans the hidden behavior power); (3) the ThisTurn tracker can create phantom negative power instances (\"Weak -1\" that still weakens) by applying -delta without checking the power still exists; (4) self-applied Vulnerable deliberately clears the engine's SkipNextDurationTick while Weak/Frail don't, so identical self-debuffs have different lifetimes; plus minor issues with hook-modified amounts desyncing ThisTurn removal (Unsettling Lamp / multiplayer scaling), TickNormally being clobbered for new player-side debuffs in the ApplyPower-by-id path, hardcoded Cleanse lists missing PowerType-based enumeration, and ApplyVulnerable dropping PowerPersistenceMode.

## [major] Artifact does not block the first application of a custom Debuff status
VANILLA: PowerCmd.Apply runs Hook.ModifyPowerAmountReceived (PowerCmd.cs:83) BEFORE power.BeforeApplied (PowerCmd.cs:88). ArtifactPower.TryModifyPowerAmountReceived zeroes the amount when GetTypeForAmount(amount)==PowerType.Debuff and the power IsVisible, then consumes a charge in AfterModifyingPowerAmountReceived (ArtifactPower.cs:17-41). Every vanilla debuff (WeakPower.cs:18 etc.) has a static Type, so the check always sees Debuff and the first application is negated.
MOD: CardEditorCustomStatusPower's Type defaults to PowerType.Buff (field init at CardEditorCustomStatusPower.cs:36, getter :58) and the user-chosen Buff/Debuff classification is only copied onto the instance inside BeforeApplied via the pending-payload (CardEditorCustomStatusPower.cs:77-81, pushed at CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25460-25461). At the moment Artifact's hook runs, the status still reports Buff, so a Debuff-classified custom status sails through Artifact and the charge is NOT consumed. On RE-application the mod calls SyncDefinition (sets Type=Debuff) before PowerCmd.ModifyAmount (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25445-25446), so stacking attempts ARE blocked - the behavior is also internally inconsistent between first apply and restack.
SCENARIO: Enemy has 1 Artifact. Playing vanilla Bash gets its Vulnerable negated and removes the Artifact charge. Playing a custom card that applies the player's own "Doomed" status (marked Debuff in the status editor, NCardEditorPopup.cs:36818) lands the full stack, leaves the Artifact charge intact, and only the second cast of the same card gets eaten by Artifact.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorCustomStatusPower.cs:36,58,77-81; CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25445-25446,25460-25462. Vanilla: Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:76-91; Core\Models\Powers\ArtifactPower.cs:17-41

## [major] Custom-status behavior effects keep firing after the status icon is removed (duration expiry / stack loss)
VANILLA: A vanilla power IS its own behavior: removing it (PowerModel.RemoveInternal, PowerModel.cs:489-494, called from PowerCmd.Remove at PowerCmd.cs:208-216, or auto-removal via ShouldRemoveDueToAmount at PowerCmd.cs:177-180) unregisters the model from the hook pipeline, so its triggered effects stop the instant the icon disappears.
MOD: The visible CardEditorCustomStatusPower and its behavior are split: behavior lives as entries on a separate invisible CardEditorExtraEffectPower (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25466-25491). Those entries are only cleaned up in ONE removal path - the RemovePower effect's ReduceConfiguredPower (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:24828-24835 -> RemoveCustomStatusBehaviorEntries :24790-24802). When the status is removed any other way - duration expiry via RevertCustomStatus (CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:354-371 calls PowerCmd.Remove/ModifyAmount directly), stacks dropping to 0 through ModifyActivePowers/negative apply (engine auto-remove, PowerCmd.cs:177-180), or a vanilla effect removing Debuff-typed powers (e.g. IllusionPower.ShouldPowerBeRemovedOnDeath, IllusionPower.cs:54-61) - the entries stay, and the execution loop RunTrigger/RunTurnBoundary has no "status still present" guard (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1884-1918, 1656-1691).
SCENARIO: A custom status "Burning: at the end of your turn, take 3 damage" with behavior effects is applied "until next turn". The icon correctly disappears at the boundary, but the player keeps taking 3 damage at the end of every turn for the rest of the combat with no visible source.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:24790-24802,24828-24835,25466-25491; CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:354-371; CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1884-1918. Vanilla: Core\Entities... PowerModel.cs:489-494; Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:177-180,208-216; Core\Models\Powers\IllusionPower.cs:54-61

## [major] ThisTurn tracker creates a phantom negative power instance (e.g. "Weak -1" that still weakens)
VANILLA: PowerCmd.Apply<T> creates a brand-new instance when none exists (PowerCmd.cs:43-49) and ApplyInternal sets any non-zero amount with no ShouldRemoveDueToAmount check (PowerModel.cs:478-487), so applying a negative amount of a power the target doesn't have produces a live power with negative stacks. No vanilla content ever does this for non-AllowNegative powers; vanilla debuff effects also apply at full strength as long as the power exists, regardless of amount (WeakPower.ModifyDamageMultiplicative, WeakPower.cs:24-46 has no Amount>0 check). Vanilla Weak ticks itself away at the end of the enemy turn (WeakPower.cs:48-54).
MOD: CardEditorTempStatTrackerPower.AfterSideTurnEnd blindly does Remove(tracker) then PowerCmd.Apply<TUnderlying>(owner, -delta, ...) with no check that the underlying power still exists (CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:39-44) - unlike the boundary-duration tracker which guards active==null (CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:315-328). Because the real WeakPower's own duration tick runs first at end of enemy turn and removes the last stack, the tracker then APPLIES a fresh WeakPower at -1. GainStatusEqualToStatus's "Lose" mode has the same hole: it calls PowerCmd.Apply with a negative amount even when the target lacks the power (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25624-25641).
SCENARIO: Play "Apply 1 Weak to an enemy - this turn". At end of the enemy turn, Weak ticks to 0 and is removed, then the tracker applies -1 Weak: the enemy shows a Weak icon with "-1" for the next full round and still deals 25% reduced damage (WeakPower only checks presence, not amount) - the "expired" debuff effectively lasts an extra turn at negative stacks.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:27-45 (esp. :39-44) vs :315-328; CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25624-25641. Vanilla: Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:43-49; Core\Models\PowerModel.cs:478-487; Core\Models\Powers\WeakPower.cs:24-54

## [minor] ThisTurn tracker records the pre-hook amount, desyncing removal when ModifyPowerAmountGiven/Received modifiers fire
VANILLA: Power amounts pass through Hook.ModifyPowerAmountGiven / ModifyPowerAmountReceived and multiplayer scaling before landing (PowerCmd.cs:79-87). Unsettling Lamp doubles debuff amounts given by the triggering card (UnsettlingLamp.cs:106-129). Vanilla temporary powers are dedicated ITemporaryPower models, so modifiers and the expiry share one amount.
MOD: For ThisTurn statuses the real power is applied through the full hook pipeline (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:36466,36472) but the invisible tracker is applied with the RAW pre-hook amount (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:36351-36354), and being an invisible Buff it is untouched by debuff modifiers. At end of turn the tracker removes only its recorded count (CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:39-44), so any hook that scaled the real application leaves leftover stacks behind permanently (or over-removes if a hook reduced it).
SCENARIO: With an Unsettling-Lamp-style doubling active, a custom card "Apply 2 Weak to an enemy - this turn" puts 4 Weak on the enemy but the tracker only logs 2; after the turn ends the enemy keeps 1-2 Weak that was supposed to be temporary (same desync occurs from multiplayer power scaling, PowerCmd.cs:84-87).
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:36351-36354,36466-36472; CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:39-44. Vanilla: Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:79-87; Core\Models\Relics\UnsettlingLamp.cs:106-129

## [major] Self-applied Vulnerable clears SkipNextDurationTick; Weak/Frail (and the vanilla engine) do not
VANILLA: PowerCmd.Apply unconditionally sets SkipNextDurationTick=true for ANY debuff landing on a player-side creature (PowerCmd.cs:100-103) - including self-inflicted ones; vanilla self-debuff cards rely on this (Doubt.cs:32, Shame.cs:34 apply Weak/Frail to the owner and let the engine flag stand, so a 1-stack self-debuff survives the next enemy-turn-end tick and still affects your following turn).
MOD: Only the ApplyVulnerable case manually clears SkipNextDurationTick for self-application during the player's turn (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27034-27059, the file's only such clear per grep); ApplyWeak (27012-27015) and ApplyFrail (27022-27025) leave the engine flag in place. So mod self-Vulnerable expires one full round earlier than the engine rule, and earlier than the mod's own self-Weak/Frail with identical settings.
SCENARIO: Build "At the end of your turn, gain 1 Vulnerable" (Doubt/Shame pattern): the Vulnerable ticks away at the very next enemy-turn end and never affects a single hit. The identical card with Weak instead (or any vanilla self-debuff like Shame's Frail) persists through your next turn. Two same-looking self-debuff cards have different durations.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27034-27059 vs 27012-27015/27022-27025 (only SkipNextDurationTick=false at :27052). Vanilla: Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:100-103; Core\Models\Cards\Doubt.cs:32; Core\Models\Cards\Shame.cs:34

## [minor] DurationTickPolicy=TickNormally is silently lost for NEW player-side debuffs applied via the generic ApplyPower(by id) effect
VANILLA: The engine sets SkipNextDurationTick=true AFTER ApplyInternal for player-side debuffs (PowerCmd.cs:91,100-103), so any flag written before Apply is overwritten.
MOD: The ApplyPower-by-id case sets the tick policy on the mutable power BEFORE PowerCmd.Apply (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26843-26845) and only re-applies it afterwards when the application merged into a pre-existing instance (!ReferenceEquals guard, :26846-26850). For a fresh application activePower==power, so TickNormally is clobbered by the engine and behaves like SuppressNextTick. The dedicated typed path does it correctly by applying the policy after Apply (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:36457-36468).
SCENARIO: Custom card "Apply 1 Frail to yourself (Tick Normally)" built with the generic ApplyPower effect (Frail's power id): the Frail lasts through your next turn anyway. The same card built with the dedicated ApplyFrail effect kind expires at the next enemy-turn end as configured - two editor routes to the same effect give different durations.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26843-26850 vs 36457-36468,36520-36546. Vanilla: Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:91,100-103

## [minor] CleanseDebuffs/CleanseBuffs remove a hardcoded 6/9-power list instead of enumerating by PowerType, missing other vanilla debuffs and custom statuses
VANILLA: Vanilla "all debuffs" effects enumerate the creature's powers by type: Misery snapshots p.TypeForCurrentAmount == PowerType.Debuff (Misery.cs:27-29) and IllusionPower strips debuffs on death via power.Type == PowerType.Debuff (IllusionPower.cs:54-61), so every debuff - No Draw, Constrict, Hex, custom-typed, etc. - is covered automatically.
MOD: CleanseDebuffs only removes Weak/Frail/Vulnerable/Poison/Doom/Constrict (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:24860-24868) and CleanseBuffs only a fixed 9-buff list (:24870-24881). Vanilla debuffs outside the list (NoDrawPower from BattleTrance.cs:23, Shackled-style powers, negative Strength) and Debuff-classified custom statuses are not removed.
SCENARIO: Player has No Draw (from Battle Trance) plus a custom "Cursed" Debuff status and plays a custom "Cleanse all debuffs from yourself" card: only the six listed statuses vanish; No Draw and the custom debuff remain, unlike any vanilla cleanse which keys off PowerType.Debuff.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:24860-24881. Vanilla: Core\Models\Cards\Misery.cs:27-29; Core\Models\Powers\IllusionPower.cs:54-61; Core\Models\Cards\BattleTrance.cs:23

## [minor] ApplyVulnerable bypasses PowerPersistenceMode tracking (asymmetric with every other status)
VANILLA: No direct vanilla analog (persistence is a mod feature); the comparison baseline is the mod's own ApplyPowerWithDuration pipeline used by all other statuses, which calls TrackBuiltInPowerPersistence (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:36318-36325, persistence at :36327-36337).
MOD: The ApplyVulnerable case was special-cased for the SkipNextDurationTick fix and now calls ApplyPower + TrackBuiltInPowerDuration directly, never TrackBuiltInPowerPersistence (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27032-27061). TrackBuiltInPowerDuration also early-returns when PowerPersistenceMode != Normal (:36343-36349), so persistence for Vulnerable is entirely dropped.
SCENARIO: Editor card "Apply 3 Vulnerable - persists for the rest of the run": the Vulnerable behaves as a normal one-combat debuff. The identical card configured with Weak persists across combats as advertised.
EVIDENCE: Mod: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27032-27061 (no persistence call) vs 36318-36337 and 36343-36349. Vanilla: n/a (mod-only knob); engine pipeline PowerCmd.cs:33-115 unaffected

### VERDICT [confirmed] Artifact does not block the first application of a custom Debuff status
CONFIRMED (major). Every cited line checks out and no compensating code exists.

VANILLA: PowerCmd.Apply runs Hook.ModifyPowerAmountReceived at Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:83 BEFORE power.BeforeApplied at :88. ArtifactPower.TryModifyPowerAmountReceived (Core\Models\Powers\ArtifactPower.cs:17-36) zeroes the amount only when canonicalPower.GetTypeForAmount(amount)==PowerType.Debuff and the power IsVisible; the charge is consumed in AfterModifyingPowerAmountReceived (ArtifactPower.cs:38-41), invoked via PowerCmd.cs:108 (apply) / :172 (ModifyAmount). PowerModel.GetTypeForAmount (Core\Models\PowerModel.cs:401-412) returns the power's Type for positive amounts, and vanilla debuffs declare it statically (WeakPower.cs:18 `Type => PowerType.Debuff`), so vanilla debuffs are always seen as Debuff at hook time.

MOD: CardEditorCustomStatusPower initializes `_customStatusType = PowerType.Buff` (CardEditorCustomStatusPower.cs:36) with `Type => _customStatusType` (:58). The user's Buff/Debuff choice (saved at NCardEditorPopup.cs:36818, carried in CardEditorCustomStatusDefinition.Type, CardEditorCustomStatusRegistry.cs:16, resolved from the saved store at :88-98) is only copied onto the instance in BeforeApplied via the AsyncLocal pending payload (CardEditorCustomStatusPower.cs:77-81 -> ApplyDefinition :140-160, pushed at CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25460 just before the Apply call at :25461). First application flows: PushPendingPayload -> CardEditorPowerCmdCompat.Apply<T> (CardEditorPowerCmdCompat.cs:126-131, a pure reflection forwarder to vanilla PowerCmd.Apply<T>) -> vanilla Apply<T> clones the canonical ModelDb instance via ToMutable() (PowerCmd.cs:43-48); the canonical is default-constructed via ModelDb.Inject (CardEditorMod.cs:2396) and nothing ever syncs a definition onto it, so at PowerCmd.cs:83 the power reports Buff. ArtifactPower.cs:24 fails the Debuff check, returns false, the power is not added to receivedModifiers, so the full stack lands and NO Artifact charge is consumed. On re-application the mod calls active.SyncDefinition(definition) (sets Type=Debuff) at CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25445 BEFORE PowerCmd.ModifyAmount at :25446, so the hook at PowerCmd.cs:164 sees Debuff, Artifact zeroes the offset and consumes a charge at :172 â€” confirming the first-apply/restack inconsistency.

REFUTATION ATTEMPTS (all failed): (1) mod-wide grep for ModifyPowerAmountReceived finds zero mod-side references â€” no Harmony patch on the hook or ArtifactPower; (2) PowerCmd_Apply_Patch / PowerCmd_ModifyAmount_Patch (CardEditorMod.cs:2640-2748) only remap amounts for card overrides, never Type; (3) CardEditorCustomStatusPowerPatches (CardEditorCustomStatusPower.cs:296-347) patch only Icon/BigIcon/HoverTips; (4) the only SyncDefinition call sites are the restack path (:25445), a power-trigger path (:25170), and a tracker (CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:356) â€” none run before the first-apply hook; (5) ApplyCustomStatusPower (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:25433-25464) has no manual Artifact check.

SCENARIO VALIDATED: vs an enemy with 1 Artifact, a custom card applying a player-defined Debuff-classified status lands its full stack and leaves the Artifact charge intact (vanilla behavior: negated, charge consumed); a second cast of the same card is then eaten by Artifact because the on-creature instance now reports Debuff. Severity: major â€” Artifact visibly fails to do its job against custom debuffs, and the rule is applied inconsistently between first apply and restack.

### VERDICT [confirmed] ThisTurn tracker creates a phantom negative power instance (e.g. "Weak -1" that still weakens)
CONFIRMED (major). Every cited line was independently re-verified and all plausible compensation paths were ruled out.

Vanilla: PowerCmd.Apply<T> (Core\Commands\PowerCmd.cs:43-49) creates a fresh mutable instance when the target lacks the power and routes it through the non-generic Apply (PowerCmd.cs:57-116), which only rejects amount==0 and calls PowerModel.ApplyInternal (Core\Models\PowerModel.cs:478-487) â€” SetAmount clamps to +/-999999999, so a -1 instance is created live. ShouldRemoveDueToAmount (PowerModel.cs:414-424; AllowNegative defaults false at PowerModel.cs:210) is only invoked from PowerCmd.ModifyAmount (~PowerCmd.cs:177), never on the fresh-instance path. WeakPower.ModifyDamageMultiplicative (Core\Models\Powers\WeakPower.cs:24-46) has no Amount>0 check â€” presence alone applies the 0.75x multiplier â€” and WeakPower.AfterSideTurnEnd (WeakPower.cs:48-54) ticks at end of Enemy side; SkipNextDurationTick is set only for Player-side targets (PowerCmd.cs ~:101), so enemy Weak ticks the same round. Hook dispatch (Core\Hooks\Hook.cs:972-996) iterates a snapshot built from creature.Powers in application order (Core\Combat\CombatState.cs:271-340): ApplyPowerWithDuration applies Weak before the tracker (mod CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:36322-36324), so Weak's self-tick runs first and the tracker still fires afterward from the snapshot.

Mod: CardEditorTempStatTrackerPower.AfterSideTurnEnd (CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:27-45, esp. 39-44) blindly does delta=Amount; Remove(this); Apply<TUnderlying>(owner,-delta) with no existence check. The PowerCmd alias is CardEditorPowerCmdCompat (line 13), verified to be a pure reflection pass-through to vanilla PowerCmd with no guard (CardEditorPowerCmdCompat.cs:119-141). The mod's own boundary-duration tracker guards this exact case (active==null -> bail unless positive revert, CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs ~:315-322), proving the omission is an oversight. GainStatusEqualToStatus Lose mode (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs ~25623-25641) has the same hole: it negates the amount and calls vanilla PowerCmd.Apply guarded only by the SOURCE status amount, never checking the target has the power being lost.

Refutation attempts that failed: (1) the Harmony prefix on PowerCmd.Remove (CardEditorPowerPersistenceTrackerPower.cs:338-369) only protects persistence-tracked powers and does not remove temp trackers when the underlying power dies; (2) DurationTickPolicy=SuppressNextTick would prevent the double-subtraction, but it defaults to Default (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:1438) and requires explicit user opt-in per effect; (3) no mod code anywhere calls ShouldRemoveDueToAmount or cleans up negative-amount powers.

Scenario verified: 'Apply 1 Weak to an enemy - this turn' with default settings -> end of enemy turn: Weak ticks 1->0 and is removed via ModifyAmount/ShouldRemoveDueToAmount; the tracker then applies a fresh WeakPower at Amount=-1 (visible icon showing -1; not in the current hook snapshot so it survives the dispatch). The enemy's powered attacks the following round deal 25% reduced damage despite the debuff having 'expired', until the phantom ticks -1 -> -2 at the next enemy turn end and is removed by ShouldRemoveDueToAmount. One caveat vs the analyst's wording: the phantom self-cleans after one round via the natural duration tick (it does not require any other cleanup), but the extra round of unearned weakness plus the visible negative stack icon are exactly as claimed. Severity: major (wrong damage numbers and a debuff visibly outliving its stated duration). The same phantom-negative-instance bug applies to all 17 temp trackers (Frail, Vulnerable, Poison, etc., CardEditorTempStatTrackerPowers.cs:48-116) for any underlying power that can hit zero before side-turn-end, and to Lose-mode status-to-status effects targeting absent powers.
