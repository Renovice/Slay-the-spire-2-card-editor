# energy-xcost-play
Audited the mod's X-cost handling and card-play lifecycle against vanilla.

**Verified-good (no divergence found):**
- **X capture moment:** vanilla captures X into `CardEnergyCost.CapturedXValue` at SpendResources (CardModel.cs:1460-1487) / AutoPlay (CardCmd.cs:82-84) before OnPlay. The mod's `ResolveXAmount` (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:23282) routes X-cost cards through `card.ResolveEnergyXValue()` which reads that same captured value, so a mod "Gain 2 Energy" row before a "Deal X damage" row does NOT change X mid-play â€” matches Whirlwind.
- **Hook.ModifyXValue (Chemical X):** respected â€” mod calls vanilla `ResolveEnergyXValue`/`ResolveStarXValue` (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:23337/23358), which apply the hook (CardModel.cs:887-907).
- **Deferred X:** scheduled/power-hosted rows snapshot X at play time via `CloneForDeferredExecution`/`CaptureDeferredXValues` (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:37581-37632; CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.cs:60; CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:151-153). RepeatIsX with X=0 resolves to 0 executions (sentinel -1, CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:37609-37614, 23584-23587) â€” matches Whirlwind X=0.
- **Repeats:** executed inside vanilla `AttackContext` (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:26247/26316/26385), same pattern as EchoingSlash/Omnislice, so per-hit hooks/Vigor behave like vanilla multi-hits.
- **Multi-play:** mod replay uses vanilla `BaseReplayCount` (CardEditorTemporaryReplayController.cs:93); extra effects re-run per play because Hook.AfterCardPlayed fires per iteration of OnPlayWrapper's loop (CardModel.cs:1536-1597).
- **Auto-play:** all mod auto-play funnels into vanilla `CardCmd.AutoPlay` (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:35060-35068) â€” Unplayable check, Hook.ShouldPlay, X capture, no energy spend; same as Mayhem-style vanilla autoplay. Exhaust routing still goes through `CardCmd.Exhaust` with its hooks.

**Divergences found:** 1 major (extra effect rows on overridden vanilla cards execute after Enchantment/Affliction OnPlay and after every AfterCardPlayed listener, instead of in OnPlay where real card text runs â€” the mod's own comments acknowledge this and only fix it for created cards), and 2 minor (Vigor consumption inconsistency between created-card and overridden-card multi-damage rows; ResultPileOverride stomping vanilla result-pile modifiers like Corruption).

## [major] Extra effect rows on overridden vanilla cards run after the entire vanilla play sequence, not where card text runs
VANILLA: A real card's printed effects run inside OnPlay, strictly before Enchantment.OnPlay, Affliction.OnPlay, and Hook.AfterCardPlayed (which fires every power/relic 'whenever you play a card' trigger). Order per play: BeforeCardPlayed -> OnPlay -> Enchantment.OnPlay -> Affliction.OnPlay -> Hook.AfterCardPlayed (CardModel.cs OnPlayWrapper). Powers like MonologuePower (gain Strength after the play) and TenderPower (lose 1 Str/Dex after each card played) therefore never affect the card's own printed damage.
MOD: For overridden vanilla cards, immediate OnPlay extra-effect rows execute in a Harmony postfix that awaits the original Hook.AfterCardPlayed task first, i.e. AFTER enchantment/affliction OnPlay and AFTER all after-play power/relic triggers (still before result-pile routing). The mod's own comments acknowledge the wrong timing ('avoids custom cards gaining Vigor ... before their own damage/effects resolve') but the fix (running rows during CardModel.OnPlay) is applied only to CardEditorCreatedCardBase cards, not to overrides of vanilla cards.
SCENARIO: Player has a Monologue-style power ('after you play <card>, gain Strength'). They play an overridden Strike with an added row 'Deal 8 damage'. A real card scripted with both lines would deal both hits with pre-gain Strength; the modded card's extra row resolves after the Strength gain and hits harder than the card text implies. Conversely with TenderPower (-1 Str/Dex after every card), the added row always deals 1 less than the printed line that just resolved. Enchantment on-play effects also fire before the card's added rows instead of after the card's text.
EVIDENCE: Vanilla: Core\Models\CardModel.cs:1561-1596 (OnPlay at 1563, Enchantment.OnPlay at 1571, Affliction.OnPlay at 1581, Hook.AfterCardPlayed at 1591); Core\Models\Powers\MonologuePower.cs:65; Core\Models\Powers\TenderPower.cs:44. Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorMod.cs:2750-2781 (Hook_AfterCardPlayed_Patch awaits original then runs extra effects), CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:12316-12407 (immediate rows executed there), self-acknowledging comments at CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:12370-12372 and 12494-12497, created-card-only fix at CardEditorMod.cs:2783-2818.

## [minor] Vigor applies to all damage rows on created cards but only the first damage on overridden vanilla cards
VANILLA: VigorPower latches one AttackCommand and zeroes itself after that command (BeforeAttack/AfterAttack); within one AttackCommand/AttackContext every hit gets the bonus (Whirlwind WithHitCount, EchoingSlash AttackContext), but a second separate attack command in the same card play gets nothing.
MOD: Created-card plays wrap multiple immediate DealDamage rows in a VigorPreserver that re-applies consumed Vigor between rows and removes the restored amount afterwards, so every row gets the full bonus. The overridden-card path (RunAfterCardPlayed) has no VigorPreserver, so the vanilla card's own attack consumes the Vigor and added damage rows (or all rows after the first) get none.
SCENARIO: With 8 Vigor (e.g. Akabeko), a custom design 'Deal 8 damage. Deal 8 damage.' deals 16+16 when built as a created custom card but only 16+8 (or 8+8 after the vanilla attack already consumed Vigor) when the same two rows are added to an overridden vanilla card â€” the identical card design produces different totals depending on how it was authored.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:12526-12547 (VigorPreserver created only in RunResolvedOnPlayEffectsDuringCardPlay), 12576-12585 and 12607-12610 (restore between rows), no equivalent in RunAfterCardPlayed (12316-12492). Vanilla: Core\Models\Powers\VigorPower.cs:29-86 (latch + removal after the latched command), Core\Models\Cards\EchoingSlash.cs:25 (single AttackContext multi-hit pattern).

## [minor] ResultPileOverride discards vanilla result-pile modifiers (Corruption exhaust, Rebound, etc.)
VANILLA: A card's intrinsic result pile (GetResultPileTypeForCardPlay, e.g. ParticleWall returning itself to a pile) is only the BASE input to Hook.ModifyCardPlayResultPileTypeAndPosition; powers like CorruptionPower then override it (skills -> Exhaust), so even self-returning vanilla cards get exhausted under Corruption.
MOD: The mod's Harmony postfix on Hook.ModifyCardPlayResultPileTypeAndPosition replaces the final chain result with the effect's configured pile whenever a ResultPileOverride row (or one-shot force-exhaust) exists, running after all vanilla power modifiers, so Corruption/Rebound-style decisions are silently thrown away.
SCENARIO: Under a Corruption-style power that exhausts skills when played, a modded skill with a 'after playing, put this on top of your draw pile' override keeps returning to the draw pile every play and can be replayed indefinitely, while every real skill (including vanilla self-returning cards) is exhausted.
EVIDENCE: Vanilla: Core\Models\CardModel.cs:1522 (base value into hook chain), Core\Hooks\Hook.cs:1080-1089, Core\Models\Powers\CorruptionPower.cs:27-38, Core\Models\Cards\ParticleWall.cs:30-32. Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:23-45 (postfix replaces __result), CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:10193-10238 (override resolution, one-shot + persistent ResultPileOverride rows).
