## Vanilla-parity audit â€” out-of-combat behavior (rewards, pools, transforms, gold/potions/HP)

Compared the card_editor mod's out-of-combat effect engine against the decompiled STS2 engine:

- **Mod side:** `CardEditorQuestEffects.cs` (out-of-combat reward switch ~382-424, AddPotionRewards/AddRelicRewards/AddCardReward 473-527), `CardEditorExtraEffects.cs` (TransformCards 29185, UpgradeDeckCards 29403, RemoveCardsFromDeck 29810, CreatePotions 35396, AddCombatCardReward 35444, BuildCardRewardCreationOptions 35481), `CardEditorRewardPools.cs` (Populate postfix injection, spec filters, RunState.CreateCard prefix), `CardEditorCreatedCardsPoolPatches.cs` (GetUnlockedCards postfix).
- **Vanilla side:** `RewardsCmd`/`RewardsSet`, `CardReward`/`PotionReward`/`RelicReward`/`GoldReward`, `CardFactory` (CreateForReward, RollForRarity, transform options), `CardRarityOdds` (pity timer), `PotionFactory`, `CardCmd.Transform`/`CardPileCmd.RemoveFromDeck`/`CardSelectCmd` selection filters, `PlayerCmd.GainGold`/`CreatureCmd.GainMaxHp`, plus vanilla baselines SpoilsMap (quest gold), DreamCatcher/PrayerWheel (out-of-combat card rewards), TheHunt (in-combat extra card reward), Alchemize (in-combat potion).

**What checks out fine:** out-of-combat quest rewards reuse vanilla Reward objects via the real `RewardsCmd.OfferCustom`, so claimed gold/potions/relics fire the vanilla hook chains (GainGold hooks, TryToProcure belt-full handling, relic queue via PullNextRelicFromFront); random transforms use vanilla `CardTransformation` + `PlayerRng.Transformations` (same rng as Claws/Leafy Poultice) with vanilla same-pool/C-U-R/no-self rules; potion rarity thresholds (10/25/65) match PotionFactory; in-combat extra card rewards mirror TheHunt's `ForRoom(roomType)` pattern; created-card pool injection rides the normal rarity-roll-then-uniform-pick path so per-card rates are vanilla.

**Divergences found (3 major, 9 minor):** Eternal cards removable and Quest cards transformable (vanilla selection filters missing, with quest map-marker corruption since Transform skips BeforeCardRemoved); out-of-combat quest potions ignoring the effect's rarity/pool filters; out-of-combat card rewards using RestSiteâ†’Other source so the rare-pity timer is neither applied nor advanced (vanilla uses Monsterâ†’Encounter even at rest sites); double generation in the CardReward.Populate postfix skewing the global pity counter; empty card rewards when a tag filter matches nothing; quest gold skippable/reordered vs SpoilsMap's direct grant; unawaited in-combat GainGold racing relic reactions; out-of-combat transforms reporting 0 to chained amounts; the RunState.CreateCard direct-replacement prefix lacking context guards; pool injection bypassing unlock epochs; and unsupported quest reward kinds silently no-opping out of combat.

## [major] RemoveCardsFromDeck can remove Eternal (unremovable) cards
VANILLA: Every vanilla deck-removal flow (shop removal, events, rest-site Cook) selects via CardSelectCmd's removal path which filters `c.IsRemovable` (cards with the Eternal keyword can never be removed); CardPileCmd.RemoveFromDeck itself performs no guard, relying on callers.
MOD: The mod's RemoveCardsFromDeck builds candidates from GetCandidatesFromConfiguredPile with no IsRemovable check anywhere (only pile/cost/selection filters), then calls CardPileCmd.RemoveFromDeck on whatever was selected, so Eternal cards are offered/picked and removed.
SCENARIO: Player has an Eternal curse (Ascender's Bane analog) in deck and plays/completes a custom card with 'Remove 1 random card from your deck' â€” the Eternal curse can be deleted, something no vanilla removal effect can ever do.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:29817-29824 (candidate build, no IsRemovable), 29874 (RemoveFromDeck). Vanilla: Core\Commands\CardSelectCmd.cs:490 (removal filter `c.IsRemovable`), Core\Models\CardModel.cs:584 (IsRemovable = !Eternal), Core\Commands\CardPileCmd.cs:42-71 (no internal guard).

## [major] TransformCards can transform Quest cards, silently breaking their quest cleanup
VANILLA: Vanilla transform selection explicitly excludes quest cards: CardSelectCmd.FromDeckForTransformation filters `c.Type != CardType.Quest && c.IsTransformable`. This matters because CardCmd.Transform removes the original via RemoveFromCurrentPile WITHOUT firing Hook.BeforeCardRemoved, and quest cards like SpoilsMap rely on BeforeCardRemoved (only fired by CardPileCmd.RemoveFromDeck) to clean up their map quest markers.
MOD: The mod's TransformCards filters candidates only by `card.IsTransformable` (quest cards pass â€” they lack Eternal), so random/chosen transforms can hit a Quest card; the quest's BeforeCardRemoved cleanup never runs because transforms bypass that hook.
SCENARIO: Player holds the Spoils Map quest card and plays a custom 'Transform a random card in your deck' effect â€” Spoils Map can be transformed into a random colorless card; the treasure quest marker stays on the act map pointing at a quest whose card no longer exists.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:29196-29204 (only IsTransformable filter). Vanilla: Core\Commands\CardSelectCmd.cs:392 (Quest excluded), Core\Commands\CardCmd.cs:305 (Transform removes without BeforeCardRemoved), Core\Models\Cards\SpoilsMap.cs:100-116 (BeforeCardRemoved map cleanup).

## [major] Out-of-combat quest potion rewards ignore all configured potion filters
VANILLA: A bare vanilla PotionReward(player) populates via PotionFactory.CreateRandomPotionOutOfCombat: any rarity (10/25/65 roll) from the character + shared pools, ignoring everything else.
MOD: When a quest completes OUT of combat, AddPotionRewards drops a generic `new PotionReward(owner)` for non-specific potions, discarding the effect's PotionRarityFilter, PotionPoolFilter, PotionInCombatOnly and PotionAllowDuplicates settings. The same effect completed IN combat honors all of those via CreatePotions/GetPotionCandidates.
SCENARIO: A quest card whose reward reads 'Create a Rare potion' completes from the map/rest site: the reward screen offers a common Shared-pool potion. The identical quest completing mid-combat correctly produces a rare from the configured pool.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorQuestEffects.cs:473-500 (generic PotionReward, filters dropped) vs mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:35396-35441 + 35538-35611 (in-combat path honoring filters). Vanilla: Core\Rewards\PotionReward.cs:54-61, Core\Factories\PotionFactory.cs:22-53.

## [minor] Out-of-combat quest card rewards bypass the rare-pity timer (RestSite/Other source)
VANILLA: Vanilla out-of-combat card rewards (Dream Catcher at rest sites, Prayer Wheel) deliberately use CardCreationOptions.ForRoom(player, RoomType.Monster) â†’ Source=Encounter, so CardFactory.RollForRarity uses CardRarityOdds.Roll: the accumulating rare-pity offset applies, grows on non-rares, and resets to -0.05 on a rare.
MOD: The quest out-of-combat AddCardReward hardcodes RoomType.RestSite â†’ ForRoom returns Source=Other, so RollForRarity takes the RollWithBaseOdds branch: accumulated pity is ignored and never advanced/reset. The same effect in combat (AddCombatCardReward) uses the real room type and participates in pity normally.
SCENARIO: Player has gone many rewards without a rare (pity offset near +0.4). A quest card reward popping on the map rolls with flat base odds â€” noticeably lower rare chance than the Dream-Catcher-style reward a vanilla design would give, and the streak counter is untouched.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorQuestEffects.cs:523 (RoomType.RestSite), mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:35481-35488. Vanilla: Core\Models\Relics\DreamCatcher.cs:22 (ForRoom Monster at rest site), Core\Runs\CardCreationOptions.cs:50-74, Core\Factories\CardFactory.cs:196-213, Core\Odds\CardRarityOdds.cs:53-65/88-100.

## [minor] Spec-filtered card rewards generate twice, skewing the global rarity pity counter
VANILLA: CardReward.Populate calls CardFactory.CreateForReward exactly once; each Encounter-source rarity roll advances the shared pity counter once per offered card, resetting it only when a rare is actually offered.
MOD: CardReward.Populate's Harmony postfix lets vanilla generate the 3 cards (consuming pity rolls), then TryApplyToGeneratedCardReward discards them and regenerates via a second CardFactory.CreateForReward (or uniform picks). Rolls for the discarded set still advanced â€” and a discarded rare still reset â€” the player-wide pity counter; ReplacePool/AddToPool injection likewise throws away pity-rolled cards.
SCENARIO: A custom card adds a combat card reward filtered to 'Attacks only'. Vanilla's hidden first pass rolls a Rare (resetting pity to -0.05), the mod regenerates and offers commons â€” the player silently lost their accumulated rare pity without ever seeing a rare, making subsequent combat rewards rarer than vanilla.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorRewardPools.cs:935-965 (Populate postfix), 727-742 (second CreateForReward), 161-178 (pool replacement after vanilla generation). Vanilla: Core\Rewards\CardReward.cs:118-137, Core\Factories\CardFactory.cs:180, Core\Odds\CardRarityOdds.cs:56-63.

## [minor] Tag/type-filtered card reward can be offered completely empty
VANILLA: Vanilla never shows an empty card reward: CardFactory.CreateForReward throws if no valid rarity/card can be generated, and CardReward.IsPopulated requires at least one card, so every offered card reward has options.
MOD: TryApplyToGeneratedCardReward, when its pool/type/rarity/custom-tag filter matches zero candidates, clears the already-generated cards and returns success â€” the reward stays in the set with zero cards.
SCENARIO: A custom card grants 'Add a card reward: cards with custom tag dragon' but no card carries that tag. After combat, the reward list shows a card-reward entry that opens a selection screen with no cards to pick.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorRewardPools.cs:658-662 and 668-672 (cards.Clear(); return true). Vanilla: Core\Factories\CardFactory.cs:183,191 (throws instead), Core\Rewards\CardReward.cs:93 (IsPopulated).

## [minor] Out-of-combat quest gold is a skippable reward and resolves after immediate gold losses
VANILLA: The vanilla quest card SpoilsMap grants its gold unconditionally and immediately on completion via `await PlayerCmd.GainGold(...)` â€” no reward screen, cannot be missed, and ordering with other effects follows card text.
MOD: The quest out-of-combat switch converts GainGold into a GoldReward offered on a rewards screen (claim required; skippable), while LoseGold in the same reward list is applied immediately via PlayerCmd.LoseGold â€” so losses always resolve before gains regardless of authored order.
SCENARIO: Quest reward 'Gain 50 Gold, Lose 20 Gold' completes on the map with the player at 0 gold: mod loses 20 first (clamped to 0) then the player claims 50 â†’ ends at 50; vanilla ordering would end at 30. A player can also back out of the reward screen and never receive quest gold at all.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorQuestEffects.cs:384-390 (GoldReward deferred, LoseGold immediate), 427-430 (OfferCustom). Vanilla: Core\Models\Cards\SpoilsMap.cs:118-124 (direct awaited GainGold), Core\Commands\PlayerCmd.cs:156 (LoseGold clamps at 0).

## [minor] In-combat GainGold effect is fire-and-forget, so gold-gain relic reactions race later effects
VANILLA: All vanilla gold grants are awaited (`await PlayerCmd.GainGold(...)`), so Hook.AfterGoldGained listeners (e.g. Dragon Fruit: +1 Max HP and heal on every gold gain) fully resolve before the next effect of the card executes.
MOD: CardEditorExtraEffects' GainGold case calls `PlayerCmd.GainGold(amount, targetPlayer);` without await (its sibling LoseGold IS awaited), so the AfterGoldGained hook chain runs concurrently with the rest of the card's effect list.
SCENARIO: With Dragon Fruit equipped, a custom card 'Gain 25 Gold, then deal damage equal to your Max HP' can compute the damage before Dragon Fruit's Max-HP gain lands (or its heal/flash plays after later effects), unlike any vanilla gold-granting card.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:27551-27557 (unawaited) vs 27559-27565 (LoseGold awaited). Vanilla: Core\Commands\PlayerCmd.cs:105-134 (async hooks inside GainGold), Core\Models\Cards\SpoilsMap.cs:120 (awaited), Core\Models\Relics\DragonFruit.cs:22-29 (AfterGoldGained reaction).

## [minor] Out-of-combat transforms report 0 applied to chained amount contexts

## [minor] Direct event-card replacement patches every RunState.CreateCard call, not just the source event
VANILLA: RunState.CreateCard(canonical, player) is the universal out-of-combat card instantiation used by reward generation (CardFactory.CreateForReward), merchant inventory (CreateForMerchant), transforms-to-specific, and event grants alike; vanilla event cards are only handed out by their own event.

EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorRewardPools.cs:575-592 (unconditional prefix), 62-96 (id-only matching). Vanilla: Core\Runs\RunState.cs:239-250 (generic CreateCard funnels here), Core\Factories\CardFactory.cs:51,64,193 (shop + reward creation through the same method).

## [minor] Pool-override injection bypasses unlock/epoch filtering of GetUnlockedCards
VANILLA: CardPoolModel.GetUnlockedCards filters the pool through unlock epochs (player progression) and the multiplayer constraint before any reward/shop/transform generation sees it.
MOD: The GetUnlockedCards postfix appends pool-overridden vanilla cards and created cards straight from ModelDb after vanilla filtering, so a vanilla card the player has not yet unlocked (epoch-locked) is offered anyway once it has a PoolTitle override pointing at the pool.

## [minor] Unsupported quest reward kinds silently do nothing out of combat
VANILLA: Vanilla quest cards only carry rewards that function where the quest completes (e.g. SpoilsMap's gold works anywhere), so a completed quest always delivers its full printed reward.
MOD: The out-of-combat switch handles only Gold/LoseGold/Potion/CardReward/RelicReward/Heal/MaxHp/Upgrade/Remove/Transform; every other configured reward kind (draw cards, apply power, energy, etc.) falls to the default case which just logs a warning â€” the player gets nothing and sees no in-game message.

EVIDENCE: Mod: mods\card_editor\CardEditorQuestEffects.cs:421-423 (default: Log.Warn only). Vanilla: Core\Models\Cards\SpoilsMap.cs:118-124 (quest reward always delivered).
