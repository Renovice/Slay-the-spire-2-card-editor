# triggers-timing
## Vanilla-parity audit: trigger timing vs vanilla hook order

Compared every Harmony postfix in `mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs` (plus the trigger runners in `CardEditorMod.cs`, `CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs`, `CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.cs`) against the vanilla dispatch order in `Core/Hooks/Hook.cs` and the turn flow in `Core/Combat/CombatManager.cs`.

**Vanilla turn flow (ground truth):** turn start = energy reset -> `BeforeHandDraw` -> draw -> `AfterPlayerTurnStart` -> `AfterSideTurnStart` (CombatManager.cs:497-531, 392). Turn end = `BeforeTurnEnd` (powers) -> orb passives -> Ethereal exhaust -> `OnTurnEndInHandWrapper` (Burn/Regret: reveal in Play pile, effect, self-discard/exhaust) -> `BeforeFlush` -> flush -> `AfterFlush` -> `AfterTurnEnd` (CombatManager.cs:966-1124).

**Checked and found OK (not reported):** OnDraw firing during the initial hand draw matches vanilla (Void.cs:27 has no fromHandDraw gate); the retained-card AfterFlush guard (`CardEditorEndOfTurnInHandTracker`, patches file 925-981/1605-1649) correctly prevents double-firing in the normal flow; `TriggerMaxTurns` counts owner-side turn ends, consistent with vanilla duration powers.

**Six divergences found (4 major, 2 minor):** the headline bugs are (1) power-hosted `EndOfTurn` entries executing twice per turn because `DoesTriggerMatch` maps the `EndOfTurnInHand` pass onto `EndOfTurn` effects and `AfterSideTurnEnd` runs both passes, which also makes "every 2nd turn" fire every turn; (2) power-hosted end-of-turn effects running post-flush (empty hand) while vanilla end-of-turn powers run pre-flush; (3) card-hosted `EndOfTurnInHand` firing one phase early (before orb passives/Ethereal) and skipping the Burn-style reveal/self-discard; (4) "start of turn" firing pre-draw or post-draw depending on whether the effect is card-hosted, power-hosted, or scheduled.

## [major] Power-hosted 'End of turn' effects execute twice every turn (and corrupt Every-N counting)
VANILLA: Vanilla dispatches each end-of-turn power exactly once per turn end: Hook.AfterTurnEnd iterates models once for AfterSideTurnEnd (Hook.cs:971-996), and end-of-turn powers/relics like Orichalcum (Orichalcum.cs:52) or Cloak Clasp (CloakClasp.cs:24) fire a single time.
MOD: CardEditorExtraEffectPower.AfterSideTurnEnd runs two back-to-back passes: RunStartOrEndTimed(EndOfTurnInHand) then RunStartOrEndTimed(EndOfTurn) (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1610-1611). DoesTriggerMatch maps observed EndOfTurnInHand onto effects whose Trigger==EndOfTurn (CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:13828-13832, intended for the card-hosted hand loop), so every power entry with the EndOfTurn trigger matches BOTH passes. There is no per-turn dedupe in RunStartOrEndTimed (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1782-1835) or ExecuteOrSchedulePowerEffect (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:869-1015). The TriggerEveryN counter also increments in both passes (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1797-1804), so the counter advances +2 per turn.
SCENARIO: Build a custom Metallicize clone: Power card with effect 'At end of turn, gain 3 Block' (AsPower). Each turn end the player visibly gains 6 Block instead of 3. With 'every 2nd turn' (TriggerEveryN=2) configured, the counter goes 1,2 within one turn end, so the effect fires EVERY turn instead of every other turn.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1610-1611, 1782-1835, 1797-1804; mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:13828-13832. Vanilla: Slay the spire 2 Source/src/Core/Hooks/Hook.cs:971-996; Core/Models/Relics/Orichalcum.cs:52; Core/Models/Relics/CloakClasp.cs:24

## [major] Power-hosted end-of-turn effects fire AFTER the hand is discarded; vanilla end-of-turn powers fire before
VANILLA: Vanilla 'at end of turn' powers/relics act in Hook.BeforeTurnEnd via BeforeSideTurnEnd, before ethereal exhausts, before Burn-type cards, and before the hand is flushed: CombatManager.EndPlayerTurnPhaseOneInternal calls Hook.BeforeTurnEnd (CombatManager.cs:966) and the flush only happens in phase two (FlushPlayerHand, CombatManager.cs:1090-1123) followed by Hook.AfterTurnEnd (CombatManager.cs:1084). E.g. Cloak Clasp 'gain Block per card in hand at end of turn' uses BeforeSideTurnEnd (CloakClasp.cs:24) so it sees the full hand.
MOD: Power entries with EndOfTurn/EndOfTurnInHand triggers are executed from the power's AfterSideTurnEnd override (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1597-1611), which vanilla dispatches from Hook.AfterTurnEnd â€” i.e., after FlushPlayerHand has moved the whole hand to discard and after Burn/ethereal processing. Note the card-hosted version of the same triggers correctly runs pre-flush at Hook.BeforeTurnEnd (CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:410-512), so the same effect text behaves differently depending on host.
SCENARIO: A custom power 'At end of turn, gain 1 Block for each card in your hand' (Cloak Clasp clone) always grants 0 Block because the hand was already flushed when it runs. Likewise 'At end of turn, discard a card' as a power does nothing, and an end-of-turn Block power lands after vanilla Burn damage resolves, so the Block never absorbs the Burn the way Orichalcum/Cloak Clasp Block would.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1597-1611. Vanilla: Slay the spire 2 Source/src/Core/Combat/CombatManager.cs:966, 1067-1084, 1090-1123; Core/Models/Relics/CloakClasp.cs:24; Core/Models/Relics/Orichalcum.cs:52

## [major] 'Start of turn' fires pre-draw for card-hosted effects but post-draw for power-hosted and scheduled effects
VANILLA: Vanilla turn-start order: energy reset (CombatManager.cs:497-505) -> Hook.BeforeHandDraw (508) -> hand draw (529) -> Hook.AfterPlayerTurnStart (531). Vanilla picks the slot per effect semantics: card-adding 'next turn' effects act pre-draw in BeforeHandDraw (NightmarePower.cs:35-47, InfiniteBladesPower.cs:20), while hand-interacting start-of-turn effects act post-draw in AfterPlayerTurnStart (ToolsOfTheTradePower.cs:27-37 discards from the freshly drawn hand, GamblingChip.cs:16).
MOD: All card-hosted StartOfTurn effects run in a Hook.BeforeHandDraw postfix, before the hand is drawn (CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:983-1046), while the exact same trigger granted as a power entry, and all scheduled 'in N turns' StartOfTurn effects, run at Hook.AfterPlayerTurnStart, after the draw (CardEditorMod.cs:2939-2974, scheduler call at 2957, power.RunStartOfTurn at 2966; CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.cs:118-166).
SCENARIO: Effect 'At the start of your turn, discard 1 card' on a card sitting in the draw pile fires before the 5-card draw â€” the hand is empty (only retained cards) so nothing is discarded; the identical effect granted as a power discards from the full drawn hand like vanilla Tools of the Trade. Similarly 'deal damage equal to cards in your hand at start of turn' computes 0 when card-hosted but ~5 when power-hosted.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:983-1046; mods/card_editor/CardEditorMod.cs:2951-2968; mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.cs:118-166. Vanilla: Slay the spire 2 Source/src/Core/Combat/CombatManager.cs:497-531; Core/Models/Powers/NightmarePower.cs:35-47; Core/Models/Powers/ToolsOfTheTradePower.cs:27-37

## [minor] Scheduled 'at end of turn' effects resolve after the hand flush and after Burn damage
VANILLA: Vanilla delayed end-of-turn effects (The Bomb) and end-of-turn Block sources (Orichalcum) act in BeforeSideTurnEnd (TheBombPower.cs:33, Orichalcum.cs:52), i.e., before orb passives, Burn-type cards, and the hand flush (Hook.BeforeTurnEnd at CombatManager.cs:966; flush at 1090-1123; Hook.AfterTurnEnd at 1084).
MOD: CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.RunAfterTurnEnd is invoked from a Hook.AfterTurnEnd postfix (CardEditorMod.cs:3144-3161; scheduler logic CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.cs:218-304), so all EndOfTurn/EndOfThisTurn/EndOfAnyTurn scheduled effects resolve after the hand has been discarded and after vanilla turn-end card damage (Burn) has already been applied.
SCENARIO: Card configured 'At the end of this turn, gain 8 Block' with two Burns in hand: a vanilla Bomb/Orichalcum-style effect would grant the Block before the Burns tick, absorbing 4 damage; the mod's scheduled Block lands after the Burns resolve, so the player takes the full Burn damage and the Block only covers the enemy turn. Hand-referencing scheduled effects ('discard a card at end of turn') also see an already-empty hand.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods/card_editor/CardEditorMod.cs:3144-3161; mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectScheduler.cs:218-304. Vanilla: Slay the spire 2 Source/src/Core/Combat/CombatManager.cs:966, 1084, 1090-1123; Core/Models/Powers/TheBombPower.cs:33; Core/Models/Relics/Orichalcum.cs:52

## [minor] Turn-end triggers fire for ALL players when only a subset ends its turn (co-op extra turns)
VANILLA: Vanilla restricts turn-end processing to the players actually ending the turn: playersEndingTurn is the extra-turn subset when an extra turn is in progress (CombatManager.cs:942-946), DoTurnEnd/flush run only for those players (973-982, 1067-1081), and Hook.BeforeTurnEnd receives exactly those participants (966).
MOD: The mod's Hook.BeforeTurnEnd postfix ignores the participants list and iterates combatState.Players, firing EndOfTurnInHand for every player's hand, EndOfTurn for every player's piles, and EndOfTurn auto-play/auto-draw for every player (CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:440-537).
SCENARIO: Co-op game: player A takes an extra turn (vanilla extra-turn flow, CombatManager.cs:1133-1139). When A's extra turn ends, player B's retained custom card with an EndOfTurnInHand effect fires a second time that round (and B's EndOfTurn pile/auto-play triggers re-run), while a vanilla Burn in B's hand correctly does nothing during A's extra turn. Single-player behavior is unaffected.
EVIDENCE: Mod: mods/card_editor/CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:440-537. Vanilla: Slay the spire 2 Source/src/Core/Combat/CombatManager.cs:942-946, 966, 973-982, 1067-1081

### VERDICT [confirmed] Power-hosted 'End of turn' effects execute twice every turn (and corrupt Every-N counting)
CONFIRMED. Every element of the claim re-verified independently; no compensating code found.

MOD SIDE (all paths re-read):
1. Double pass: CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1607-1611 â€” when side == owner.Side, AfterSideTurnEnd awaits RunStartOrEndTimed(EndOfTurnInHand) then RunStartOrEndTimed(EndOfTurn) back-to-back.
2. Cross-match: CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:13828-13832 â€” DoesTriggerMatch returns true for (observedTrigger==EndOfTurnInHand && effect.Trigger==EndOfTurn). This mapping was built for the card-hosted hand loop (RunEndOfTurnInHand, CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:12737-12739, which iterates only cards in hand), but the power path (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1791) calls the same 3-arg DoesTriggerMatch with NO pile/location check. So a power entry with Trigger==EndOfTurn matches pass 1 via the mapping AND pass 2 via direct equality. (Asymmetry note: entries with Trigger==EndOfTurnInHand match only pass 1 and are fine; only EndOfTurn entries double-fire.)
3. No dedupe anywhere: RunStartOrEndTimed (1772-1841) has no per-turn tracking. ExecuteOrSchedulePowerEffect (869-1015) has only two guards, neither compensating: ShouldSuppressEffect (CardEditorAutoPlayLoopGuard.cs:318-356) suppresses only recursive self-loops via a token stack that is empty at a top-level turn-end hook; GetRemainingEffectUses (CardEditorAutoPlayLoopGuard.cs:272-306) is active only when the user sets AutoPlayLoopLimit>0 (default returns -1 = unlimited). CardEditorEndOfTurnInHandTracker (CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:1605) dedupes only the card-hosted hand loop, not the power. No Harmony patch references CardEditorExtraEffectPower in the trigger patches. No trigger remapping on entry insertion: AddPowerEffects (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:133-186) clones the effect with its original Trigger; the only `.Trigger =` reassignments in the mod set OnPlay in unrelated display/runnable paths.
4. EveryN corruption: CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1797-1804 â€” entry.TriggerCounter++ executes in BOTH matched passes (it runs after DoesTriggerMatch, before execution), so the counter advances +2 per player turn end. With TriggerEveryN=2: pass 1 counter=1 (skip), pass 2 counter=2 (fire) â€” fires every turn instead of every other turn, exactly as claimed. Non-Immediate timing is also doubled (two Scheduler.Schedule calls).
5. Scenario buildable: EndOfTurn is a selectable timed power trigger (NCardEditorPopup.cs:24727-24729) and Block kind is AsPower-eligible (SupportsAsPower exclusion list, CardEditorExtraEffects.cs:5224-5239 â€” Block not excluded).

VANILLA SIDE: Hook.cs:971-996 (AfterTurnEnd) iterates combat hook listeners exactly once for AfterSideTurnEnd per turn end (the second loop at 988-995 calls a different method, AfterSideTurnEndLate, which the mod power does not use for these effects). Orichalcum (Orichalcum.cs:52, technically BeforeSideTurnEnd with a one-shot latch at 54-59) fires once per turn end â€” minor citation nit (Before vs After hook) but the substance is correct: vanilla end-of-turn power/relic effects fire exactly once per turn end.

RESULT: A custom power 'At end of turn, gain 3 Block' visibly grants 6 Block per turn (two separate executions within one turn end), and 'every 2nd turn' fires every turn. Severity major (wrong numbers, broken every-N cadence). Only refinement: the doubling affects player-side turn end only; the enemy-turn path (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1633-1638) runs a single EndOfEnemyTurn pass and is unaffected.

### VERDICT [confirmed] Power-hosted end-of-turn effects fire AFTER the hand is discarded; vanilla end-of-turn powers fire before
CONFIRMED (major). Every cited line checks out and no compensating code exists.

VANILLA SIDE (verified): CombatManager.EndPlayerTurnPhaseOneInternal calls Hook.BeforeTurnEnd at CombatManager.cs:966; Hook.BeforeTurnEnd dispatches model.BeforeSideTurnEnd at Hook.cs:964 (plus VeryEarly/Early variants at 950/957). Only afterwards does DoTurnEnd (CombatManager.cs:998-1028) exhaust Ethereal cards (1022) and run Burn-type OnTurnEndInHandWrapper (1026). The hand flush happens in phase two: FlushPlayerHand loop at 1067-1081, method at 1090-1123 (cards moved to Discard at 1118), and Hook.AfterTurnEnd fires at 1084, dispatching AfterSideTurnEnd at Hook.cs:982. Vanilla end-of-turn content confirms this is the convention: CloakClasp.cs:24 (Block per card in hand) and Orichalcum.cs:38/52 both use BeforeSideTurnEnd(VeryEarly) â€” pre-flush, pre-Burn.

MOD SIDE (verified): CardEditorExtraEffectPower overrides only AfterSideTurnEnd (CardEditorExtraEffectPower.cs:1597); for the owner's side it runs RunStartOrEndTimed(EndOfTurnInHand) and RunStartOrEndTimed(EndOfTurn) at lines 1610-1611. RunStartOrEndTimed (1772-1841) executes the entries immediately at that point via ExecuteOrSchedulePowerEffect â€” i.e., after FlushPlayerHand and after Burn/Ethereal processing. A grep for BeforeSideTurnEnd across the entire mod returns ZERO matches, and lines 1610/1611 are the ONLY call sites for these two triggers in power mode.

### VERDICT [confirmed] Card EndOfTurnInHand fires before orb end-of-turn passives and Ethereal, and skips the vanilla reveal/self-discard sequence
CONFIRMED. Both sides of the evidence verify exactly, and no compensating mod code exists.

Vanilla: CombatManager.cs:966 awaits Hook.BeforeTurnEnd, then DoTurnEnd (978, body at 998-1027) runs in order: OrbQueue.BeforeTurnEnd (1000, which drives FrostOrb.cs:35 GainBlock via OrbQueue.cs:81-102), a hand scan where HasTurnEndInHandEffect cards are excluded from Ethereal (if/else-if at 1011-1018), Ethereal exhausts (1020-1022), then OnTurnEndInHandWrapper (1024-1027). CardModel.cs:1353-1369 confirms the wrapper reveals the card in the Play pile, waits, runs the effect, then self-exhausts (Ethereal) or self-discards. Burn.cs:22 sets HasTurnEndInHandEffect=true with damage at 31-36.

Mod: CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:410-468 is a Harmony postfix on Hook.BeforeTurnEnd that chains its EndOfTurnInHand execution onto the hook's returned Task (__result). Because CombatManager awaits that exact task at line 966, all mod EndOfTurnInHand effects complete BEFORE DoTurnEnd runs â€” i.e., before orb end-of-turn passives, before Ethereal, and before vanilla turn-end-in-hand cards. The card stays in hand (no Play-pile reveal, no self-discard/exhaust by the mod) and is flushed normally later; if it has Ethereal, the vanilla pass at CombatManager.cs:1015-1022 exhausts it after its effect already ran (since HasTurnEndInHandEffect is false for mod cards).

Refutation attempts all failed: zero occurrences of HasTurnEndInHandEffect, OnTurnEndInHandWrapper, or DoTurnEnd anywhere in mods/card_editor; a sweep of HarmonyPatch targets shows no patches on CombatManager or OrbQueue; the two CardPileCmd.Add(card, PileType.Play) calls in CardEditorExtraEffects.cs (30088, 35110) are auto-play/generated-card paths unrelated to this trigger; the Hook.AfterFlush patch (CardEditorExtraEffectTriggerPatches.cs:925-981) plus CardEditorEndOfTurnInHandTracker (1605-1639) is only a retained-card fallback/double-fire guard, firing even later, not at the vanilla point.

The Frost-orb scenario plays out as described: Frost block is granted in DoTurnEnd after Hook.BeforeTurnEnd, so a mod 'take 2 damage if in hand at end of turn' card deals its damage before the block exists, costing real HP, whereas vanilla Burn's damage (run at CombatManager.cs:1026) would be absorbed. The cosmetic divergence (no Burn-style pop into the play area) is also real.

Severity: major â€” wrong trigger ordering relative to orb passives/Ethereal/vanilla turn-end cards produces observable HP differences, plus the missing reveal/self-discard sequence is player-visible.
